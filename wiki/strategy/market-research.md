# Market research

Research into the market for a modern reference on the Moldavian Bull's Head
(*Cap de bour*) 1858 stamps. Feeds [[vision-mission-objectives]],
[[target-audience]], and [[multilanguage-analysis]]. Prepared 2026-07-04.

## Executive summary

A **small, high-value, expert, multilingual niche.** The stamps are investment-grade
classics (examples routinely €5k–€100k; a record item ~€750k–830k [8][9][19]), but the
serious global collector base is **low hundreds**, not thousands [16][25]. The
definitive reference, **Heimbüchler**, is **German/English bilingual, out of print,
~€300** [21][31]. The deepest Cap-de-bour monograph, **Dragomir 2008**, is
**Romanian-only, 250 copies** [34]. The clear gap: a **modern, accessible, fully
illustrated English-forward reference** that consolidates **forgery/authentication**
guidance and **cultural history** — neither well served today. On language: field norm
is **bilingual (a scholarly language + English)**, occasionally trilingual; **no
four-language precedent found**; French adds least ([[multilanguage-analysis]]).

## ⚑ Two findings that need the author's attention

> **These are recorded as tasks/flags because they touch credibility (REQ-C3, DR-007).**

1. **The handstamp codes `PM52, M1, M2, M3, C1` were not found in any public source.**
   The public taxonomy for these cancellations is **Kiriac (Dragomir) figure numbers**
   [1][5]. This is a *positive* signal (the book's granular taxonomy is not freely
   available) **but it must be sourced** — is it Heimbüchler-derived, Kiriac-mapped, or
   original to this author? Whatever the answer, state it, and give a concordance to
   the Kiriac figure numbers so readers can cross-reference. → see [[../entities/handstamps/PM52]].
2. **Several references cited in the book could not be verified** and may not exist as
   described: a **"Dragomir & Nazâru" joint Bull's Head catalogue**, the **French
   "Marcel Danesco" catalogue**, and a **Kalman Illyefalvi** Bull's Head work [37].
   Verify each before citing; if unverifiable, drop or mark `status: unverified` in
   [[../entities/references]].

## Competitive landscape

The literature is **fragmented across German, Romanian, French, and (thinly) English**,
with **no modern standalone English work** [37][38].

| Work | Language | Scope | Status / price | Gap it leaves |
|------|----------|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Heimbüchler, *Die Ochsenköpfe der Moldau*** (Bd 1 1994/95 418pp; Bd 2 2002; 3rd Suppl. 2007) [30][31][32][33] | **DE + EN** bilingual | The definitive opus; 1000+ images, 22 colour plates | **Out of print**; Bd 1 €200, set ~€300 | OOP, costly, multi-volume; bilingual not native EN; ~20–30 yrs old, predates recent finds |
| **Dragomir, *Monografia … Cap de bour, em. I 1858*** (2008, 291pp) [34] | **RO only** | Deepest Cap-de-bour-specific monograph | **250 numbered copies** | Inaccessible to non-Romanian readers; scarce |
| **Dragomir, *Ștampilografie poștală* 1990** [1][5] | RO | Handstamps; "Kiriac figure" numbers = de-facto public cancel reference | OOP | The public cancel taxonomy — map to it |
| Dragomir, *Catalogul mărcilor poștale românești* (1974, 623pp) [39] | RO | Reference catalogue incl. forgery marks | OOP/antiquarian | Language-locked |
| Older English: Billig Vol. XIII, Russell 1918, Williams 1993 [37][10] | EN | General/rarity coverage | Old | No modern specialist EN treatment |
| French: Cohen (1945–52), Brunel [37] | FR | Essays/studies | Old | — |
| German forgery: Billig & Stiedl, *Handbuch der Fälschungen — Rumänien* [37] | DE | Forgeries | OOP | No modern **English** forgery reference |
| Auction monographs: Köhler "Vol. 12 — Heimbüchler Collection"; Feldman/Corinphila sale catalogues [3][37] | mixed | Reference-grade catalogues | — | — |

**Net gap:** (a) no modern, affordable, single-volume, comprehensive **English**
treatment; (b) deepest works are OOP, scarce, language-locked; (c) **no consolidated
modern English forgery reference** despite these being among the most-forged classics.
**This whitespace is the book's opportunity.**

## Market size & channels

- **Serious core:** low hundreds globally. Hard proxy: **ARGE Rumänien = 27 active
  members from 8 countries (Jan 2025)** [24] — understates the pool but shows how narrow
  the hard core is. Wider penumbra: a few thousand Romania/East-Europe topical
  collectors + diaspora/heritage. Broader hobby aging (APS ~24,000, down from ~57,800 in
  1988) [17].
- **The money is real:** 81 parale blue $50,132 (Corinphila 2013); 81 parale €42,700
  (Feldman 2019); 108 parale unused $34,217 (2018); three unused heads €92,000 (2015);
  27 parale €26,000 (Artmark 2015); the *Zimbrulu și Vulturulu* "Large Journal" ~€750k–830k
  [8][9][12][18][19]. General band €5k–€100k [1].
- **Societies:** ARGE Rumänien (DE/CH, est. 1969) [24]; Society for Romanian Philately
  (US, *Romanian Philatelic Studies*, EN) [27][28]; ROMFILATELIA (RO state issuer) [29];
  Romanian national federation / EFIRO (FEPA) [29].
- **Channels (low-volume, high-relevance):** specialist philatelic-literature dealers
  (Leonard Hartmann/Philatelic Bibliopole, Vaccari) [22][23]; study-society member
  networks [24][27]; auction-house audiences/catalogue cross-sell (Feldman, Corinphila,
  Köhler, Spink) [3][8]; direct-to-collector (author site, as Heimbüchler does) [16];
  AbeBooks/eBay for resale; Amazon/POD viable but weak on *discovery*; institutional
  (APS library, museums/national libraries) [25].

## Pricing & format expectations

- **Benchmark:** Heimbüchler Bd 1 €200 / set ~€300 [21][31]; RPSL specialist hardbacks
  £50–£200 (leather tiers to £360); Vaccari hardbound colour ~€78 [format sources].
- **Realistic price:** a new image-heavy hardcover ≈ **€150–€200 (~$160–$220)**, premium
  tier **€300–€400**.
- **Format:** **hardcover with high-quality colour plates is essential** — for a
  plating/forgery study, colour fidelity *is* the value. **PDF is a secondary add-on**
  (searchability/reach), not a substitute.
- **Print run:** hundreds to low thousands (**300–1,000 normal**; Dragomir was 250 [34]).
  Niche philatelic books go OOP and become collectible — **plan for no reprint; the
  first edition must be right.**
- **Recognition path:** design to FIP Literature Class / FEPA study-award standards; an
  **English edition widens eligibility and reviewer reach** (Crawford Medal tier).

## Key uncertainties

- **Census conflicts** (687/714/724/750/"over 800") — cite Heimbüchler's census and
  present a **range**, mark `debated` [1][2][16].
- **PM52/M1–M3/C1 not in public sources** — confirm derivation (see ⚑ above) [1][5].
- **Dragomir & Nazâru / French Danesco / Illyefalvi — unverified**, possibly nonexistent
  as described [37].
- **"Aurochs vs. zimbru/bison" is genuinely contested** — content opportunity, mark
  `debated` [26] (see [[../entities/concepts/aurochs]]).
- No firm count of serious collectors; ARGE's 27 is the only hard figure [24]. Some
  auction write-ups are promotional.
