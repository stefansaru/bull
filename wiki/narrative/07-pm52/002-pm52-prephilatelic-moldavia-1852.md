## ⨀ PM52 Prephilatelic Moldavia 📅 1852

<!-- u:u00724 kind:text -->
⏭ 📖 ⨀ Kiriac, p.70

<!-- u:u00725 kind:quad -->
Prephilatelic Moldavia 📅 1852
The linear engraved PM52 have a simple design, consisting of a rectangular frame with rounded corners, enclosing the word ФPAHKO (FRANCO) in capital Cyrillic letters.
The rectangular frame varies: length between 28 and 35 mm and width between 4 and 5.5 mm. Letter height varies between 6 and 9.5 mm. The distinctive elements of the engraving and the letters' design characterize each PM52. The PM52 can be placed either vertically or horizontally, depending on the location of the mailing office.
The ink is generally black, and rarely dark greenish blue.
Simple, rudimentarily engraved handstamps and lower quality ink, generally black (rarely dark blue-green), have sometimes given impressions that are too smudged or diffuse (blurred, washed out), discolored or partially applied.
These handstamps are preferred on letters (envelopes/covers) from which the place of dispatch and possibly the date can be determined.
