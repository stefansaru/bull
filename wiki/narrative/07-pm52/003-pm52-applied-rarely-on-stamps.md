## ⨀ PM52 Applied rarely on stamps

<!-- u:u00727 kind:quad -->
Applied rarely on stamps
The pre-philatelic P52 handstamps were not found on the stamps from the first issue, although the instruction was given that they should also be canceled "with half of the franc[o] so that they could no longer remain valid".
The P52 handstamps were applied, very rarely, on stamps. There have been letters with 40 Parale stamps from the second issue and canceled with the P52 handstamp.
