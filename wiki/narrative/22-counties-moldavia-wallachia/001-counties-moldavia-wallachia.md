# Counties 🌍 Moldavia, Wallachia

<!-- u:u01520 kind:quad -->
The term "județ" (plural: "județe") for administrative divisions in Romania has roots that go back to medieval times. It referred to an administrative region governed by a "jude" (judge or leader), which is similar to a county.
