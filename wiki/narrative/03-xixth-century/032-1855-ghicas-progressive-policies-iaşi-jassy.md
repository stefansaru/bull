## 1855 📅 Ghica’s progressive policies 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00357 kind:quad -->
Ghica’s progressive policies
Grigore Ghica strongly supported the Partida Națională, the pro-unionist party pushing for the unification of Moldavia and Wallachia. Ghica also promoted freedom of the press, encouraging new publications to spread ideas and support the unionist cause. One of his key achievements was the abolition of Roma slavery. Ghica freed the Roma slaves and compensated their owners with state bonds, ensuring economic stability. Additionally, he introduced postal reforms influenced by France's revenue stamp system. Ghica's progressive policies played a key role in paving the way for the unification of the Romanian principalities. However, his reforms also drew hostility from anti-unionist factions, including Austria and the Ottoman Empire, both of whom grew increasingly dissatisfied with his pro-union stance and reformist agenda.
