## 1856 📅 „Unirea” Society 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00422 kind:quad -->
„Unirea” Society
On May 25, 1856, in Iasi, the capital of Moldavia, the "Unirea" Society was founded, marking an important step in the movement for the unification of the Romanian Principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia. This society was set up to promote the union of the two principalities, an issue which had gained increasing momentum following the end of the Crimean War and the Treaty of Paris (1856), which left the future of the Romanian Lands under European supervision.
