## 1857 📅 📯 New ⨀ M1 Handstamps 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00450 kind:quad -->
Purchase ⨀ M1
In the spring of 1857, Teodor Ghica, the postal service contractor in Moldova, purchased handstamps engraved by J. Radnitzky from a supplier in Vienna. These appear in the inventory dated November 10, 1857, used to record their cost.
On August 4, 1858, the handstamps were officially distributed from the central office in Iași to regional post offices for use in cancelling the first Cap de Bour stamps. However, some offices had already received M1-type handstamps before the official distribution, indicating earlier partial use.
