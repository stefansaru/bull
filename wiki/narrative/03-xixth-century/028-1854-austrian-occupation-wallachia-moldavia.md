## 1854 📅 Austrian occupation 🌍 Wallachia, Moldavia

<!-- u:u00346 kind:quad -->
Austrian occupation
March 1854 📅 Britain and France, aiming to curb Russian expansion and protect their interests, declare war on Russia, aligning with the Ottomans. 
June 1854 📅 Russian troops prepare to storm Silistra but withdraw after receiving orders, as Austria demands Russian withdrawal to prevent their strengthened presence in the Balkans from encouraging Slavic revolts within its own empire. 
Based on the agreement from Boiagi-Kioi (2/14 June 1854) between Austria and the Porte, Austria temporarily occupies the Danubian Principalities in order to ensure Russian withdrawal.
