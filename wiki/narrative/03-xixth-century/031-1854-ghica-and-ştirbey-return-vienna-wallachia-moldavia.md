## 1854 📅 Ghica and Ştirbey return 🌍 Vienna  Wallachia, Moldavia

<!-- u:u00355 kind:quad -->
Ghica and Ştirbey return 
October 1854 📅 Grigore Ghica returned to Moldavia following an invitation from the Ottomans. Likewise, Barbu Știrbey returned to Wallachia.
