## 1856 📅 Still under Austrian occupation 🌍 Moldavia, Wallachia

<!-- u:u00424 kind:quad -->
Still under Austrian occupation
After the Crimean War, Austria controlled Moldavia and Wallachia, opposing their unification. Austria kept the principalities weak and divided, exploiting their resources while maintaining Ottoman control to prevent the balance of power in Eastern Europe from shifting. Austria's fear of revolutionary nationalism was key to its opposition. A united Romanian state could inspire Romanians in Transylvania, under Austrian rule, to seek autonomy or independence.
