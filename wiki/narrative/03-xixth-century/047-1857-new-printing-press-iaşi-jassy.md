## 1857 📅 📯 New printing press 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00448 kind:quad -->
New printing press
A new printing press 'with balancer and counter' was purchased in March 1857 from Vienna. For its installation in A.T. Iaşi, 'a table with iron legs' was made (A.S.B. fund M.F. dos. 394/1857).
