## 1852 📅 📯 Directorate and Office of the Diligences 🌍 Moldova

<!-- u:u00333 kind:quad -->
Directorate and Office of the Diligences
In 1852, the "Directorate and Office of the Diligences" was founded in Iași. The following excerpt is from contract 9805/1852 between the government and the postal service entrepreneur.
"... the provision of administration, as well as the organization of employees and payment of salaries, the procurement of diligences and necessary materials are the responsibility of the enterprise. The diligences will leave from Iași towards both borders twice a week, transporting not only passengers but also letters, official and private correspondence. (I) 
All summonses from courts, large and small, are to be carried free of charge, except for sums of money from the treasury. (V) 
The post of private letters is to be arranged by this enterprise. (VI) 
Private letters are subject to the payment of taxes established by the Ordinance No. 511 of February 23, 1852, exclusively to the service of the expedition."
