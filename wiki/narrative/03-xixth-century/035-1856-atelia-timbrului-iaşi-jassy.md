Stamp’s Workshop
The Stamp Workshop, established in Iași by Grigore Ghica, was created to produce the "Timbru Domnesc" (Princely Stamp). Initially, the workshop had a larger staff, but after a review, several positions were deemed unnecessary and left vacant.
The revised structure included 1 Director, 1 Secretary (Alecu Duka from the Ministry of Finance), and 4 employees. The employees were divided into two pairs: a senior-assistant pair for administrative tasks and another for paper handling and printing. Kostacki Kociu, the senior paper handler, was responsible for receiving and verifying stamp paper sheets.
To ensure accountability, each role required a financial guarantee: the Secretary had to deposit 1,000 ducats, the senior paper handler 2,000 ducats, and other employees 500 ducats each. 
Monthly salaries were as follows: the Secretary 500 lei, the senior paper handler 600 lei, his assistant 150 lei, and other employees 250 lei each.
