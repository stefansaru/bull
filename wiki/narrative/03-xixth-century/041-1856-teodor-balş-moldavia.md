## 1856 📅 Teodor Balş 🌍 Moldavia

<!-- u:u00426 kind:text -->
1856-07-08  1857-02-17    J 📅 G    1856-07-20  1857-03-01

<!-- u:u00427 kind:quad -->
The period between the end of Ghica V’s reign in Moldavia and the rise of Nicolae Vogoride was a time of political turbulence, defined by internal power struggles, foreign intervention, and growing tension over the unification of the Romanian principalities. After Ghica V's deposition, Teodor Balș briefly took over as caimacam (temporary governor), but his tenure was marked by his staunch opposition to the idea of unification. A supporter of both Austrian and Ottoman interests, Balș worked to preserve the status quo, aligning himself with those powers who sought to prevent the union of Moldavia and Wallachia. His actions were often cloaked in secrecy, and he relied on a network of agents and hidden channels to maintain influence, a strategy in which he was supported by his wife, known for her cunning and talent for intrigue and manipulation. As a result, Teodor Balș became the figurehead of the conservative faction, alienating those who had embraced the hope of a brighter, united future for the principalities.
