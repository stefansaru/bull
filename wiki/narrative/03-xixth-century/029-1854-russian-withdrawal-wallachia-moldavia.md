## 1854 📅 Russian withdrawal 🌍 Wallachia, Moldavia

<!-- u:u00348 kind:quad -->
Russian withdrawal
Tsar Nicholas I, facing a potential Austrian entry into the war, orders Russian troops to retreat.
With the Principalities now at peace, the war shifts to Crimea, leaving Austria in military control of Moldavia and Wallachia.
