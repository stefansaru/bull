## 1857 📅 📯 New Postal Headquarters 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00453 kind:quad -->
New Postal Headquarters
Orfanotropie refers to "orphan care" or an "institution for orphan care." In the 18th and 19th centuries, these institutions were dedicated to the social and medical protection of orphans and newborns, providing them with shelter, care, and assistance. Such an institution, known as the Institutul de Orfanotropie, existed in Iași in 1857.
Theodor Ghica was a member of the influential Ghica family, which included Prince Grigore Ghica. Although Theodor was not a reigning prince, he was involved in Moldavian postal services and was a key entrepreneur in the sector.
On April 23, 1857, Kostache Bosie signed a contract with Theodor Ghica, renting 14 rooms in several of his houses in Iași, located across from the Orphanotropy Institute. 
The annual rent was set at 300 galbeni (gold coins), and the space would serve as the Central Postal Office following the 1858 postal reform.
