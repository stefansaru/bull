## 1852 📅 📯 Single postal tariff 🌍 Moldavia

<!-- u:u00328 kind:text -->
📅 1852 🌍 Moldavia

<!-- u:u00329 kind:quad -->
Single tariff
The postal services in Moldova and Wallachia were not only confined within their borders but also faced competition from the foreign postal services of neighboring states.
In Moldova, the postal service was leased to entrepreneurs through contracts lasting 3 to 5 years. These entrepreneurs charged fees for transporting private correspondence based on weight and distance. These fees often reached prohibitively high amounts, and the transport could take several weeks, leading the majority of the population to refrain from sending letters. 
Under these conditions, in 1852 Moldova became one of the first states to adopt the unique postal tariff. Letters were now submitted at the post office with a fee of 40 parale per sheet of paper. The sheets were no longer weighed and the same fee applied regardless of distance. To indicate that the fee had been paid, the FRANCO mark was applied using PM52 handstamps.
In Moldova, four postal route zones were established, each assigned to a postal contractor: Nicolae Mavrocordat, Costache Moruzi, Teodor Ghica, Nicolae Milo.
