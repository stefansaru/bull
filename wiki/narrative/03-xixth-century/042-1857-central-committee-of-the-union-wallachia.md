## 1857 📅 „Central Committee of the Union” 🌍 Wallachia

<!-- u:u00429 kind:text -->
1857-03-03    J 📅 G    1857-03-15

<!-- u:u00430 kind:quad -->
„Central Committee of the Union”
The 'Central Committee of the Union' was established in Bucharest with the purpose of coordinating organized actions aimed at the Union of the Principalities on all levels. On September 7 of that year, elections were held in Wallachia for the ad-hoc assembly, with the unionists emerging as the winners. Culturally, more and more organizations supporting the unionist movement began to form. Also in March 1857, the unionist newspaper 'Concordia' was launched in Bucharest.
