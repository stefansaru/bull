## 1857 📅 Austrian withdrawal 🌍 Wallachia, Moldavia

<!-- u:u00432 kind:quad -->
Austrian withdrawal
The occupation ended in 1857 due to international pressure, particularly from France and Britain, who favored autonomy and unification. Austria’s withdrawal allowed the pro-unionist movement to gain strength.
