## Pantone

<!-- u:u01593 kind:quad -->
The majority of these colors are referred to using a three- or four-digit number followed by a C or U. The letter suffix refers to the paper stock on which it is printed: a "C" for coated or gloss paper or a "U" for uncoated paper. Pantone Process Color numbers start with the letter P followed by a one- to three-digit number, a dash, and a one- to two-digit number.
