## Why write this book?

<!-- u:u00017 kind:quad -->
Why write this book?
While the first Romanian stamps seem quite rudimentary at first glance, understanding the context in which they were issued is quite a challenge. Despite the many excellent books out there, finding all the necessary information to truly understand these primitive stamps can be difficult. For instance, the aurochs, the animal depicted on the stamp—what it was, where it lived, and what it looked like—is not easily accessible in most philatelic resources. Similarly, the 19th century was a complex time of war and struggle, and understanding that context is key to appreciating the stamps’ significance. This book is the result of extensive research into the context of these stamps. It not only covers the stamps themselves but also explores the environment in which they were created. You'll find details about the stamps, examples of fakes, the postmarks used, and even some key references that bring the 19th-century backdrop to life. While some of these aspects may not seem directly related to the stamps at first, they offer crucial insight into the world these stamps were born in. By weaving together these historical, social, and cultural threads, this book aims to provide you with a deeper understanding of these fascinating, albeit simple, stamps and the period they represent.
