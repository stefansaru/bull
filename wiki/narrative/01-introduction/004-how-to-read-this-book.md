## How to read this book?

<!-- u:u00019 kind:quad -->
How to read this book?
This book is designed to appeal to a broad audience, whether you’re a seasoned stamp collector, a history enthusiast, or someone just curious about Romania’s first postal stamps. 
Throughout the book, you'll encounter a variety of symbols, which are explained in §1. While readers may come from different backgrounds, the structure of the book allows everyone to find sections of particular interest and relevance. To navigate the content effectively, it is advisable to consult the section list at the very end of the book, where you can easily find topics that pique your curiosity. 
However, it is recommended to first read §2, which talks about the aurochs. This understanding forms an essential foundation for appreciating the historical and cultural weight of these stamps. 
Next, move on to §3 for a perspective on the context of the Romanian principalities during the first half of the nineteenth century, providing insight into the political and social landscape in which these stamps were born.
From §4, which covers 1858, the book shifts its focus more toward the stamps themselves, including an explanation of their manufacturing process. 
Next, §5 explores 1859, the year of the Union of the Principalities, shedding light on this tremendous achievement and its impact on national identity. 
The book is structured into three parts: the first explores the historical context and symbolic significance, the second delves into the technical aspects of the stamps, such as the postmarks used, a detailed look at each stamp issue, and examples of both authentic stamps and replicas. 
Towards the end of the book, you’ll find a third part with additional useful information, such as a listing of color names, a set of referenced works, and a detailed list of figures. This last section also contains some other fascinating details for those eager to dive deeper into the world of early Romanian philately.
