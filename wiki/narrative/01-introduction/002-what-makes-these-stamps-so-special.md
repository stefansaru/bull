## What makes these stamps so special?

<!-- u:u00015 kind:quad -->
What makes these stamps so special?
The first Romanian postal stamps from 1858-1862 are fascinating historical artifacts. At first glance, they may appear unimpressive—simple designs, unclear text, and even the paper quality is often poor. So, why are these stamps so highly valued by collectors?
Their appeal comes from their historical significance, not their artistic quality. These stamps were issued during a critical period when the Romanian principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia were moving toward unification, while still under foreign occupation and paying tribute to the Ottoman Empire. They represent the growing desire for independence and unity, making them symbols of national identity rather than just postal items.
Despite their simplicity, these stamps are quite complicated to study. The first Moldavian stamps were heavily forged, making it difficult to identify authentic examples. Surprisingly, even some contemporary forgeries hold significant value, adding to their mystique. There’s also ambiguity over the animal depicted on the stamp—whether it's an aurochs or a wisent, which adds further intrigue.
