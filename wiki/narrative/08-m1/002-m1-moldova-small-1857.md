## ⨀ M1 Moldova small 📅 1857

<!-- u:u00802 kind:text -->
📖 Kiriac 1974, p.33

<!-- u:u00803 kind:text -->
📖 ⨀ Kiriac, p.80

<!-- u:u00804 kind:quad -->
Round handstamp, double circle, with variable dimensions (20.2 - 21.5/12.2 - 13 mm). In the exergue, the location of the post office (above) and the word "MOLDOVA" (below), uppercase antique characters; in the middle of the small circle, the date, in Arabic numerals, in the form of a fraction, with the numerator being the day and the denominator the month, separated by a small line (11 mm).
