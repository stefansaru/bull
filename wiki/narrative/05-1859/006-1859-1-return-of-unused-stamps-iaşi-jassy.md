## 1859 📅 🐃 1: Return of Unused Stamps 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00715 kind:text -->
1859-03-10    J📅G    1859-03-22

<!-- u:u00716 kind:quad -->
Return of Unused Stamps
The postage stamps recorded in the investigation journal were taken over by the new management of the Postal Directorate, led by director Costache Magistan, and sent to the Ministry of Finance for the settlement of the account, through document no. 548 dated February 26, 1859.
This document mentions that, upon opening the chest of the late cashier Neculai Costin, 12,308 stamps from the first Cap de Bour issue were found. The stamps were stored in the archive cabinet in sealed packages, sealed by the Ministry of Finance.
The document includes a resolution dated March 5, 1859, ordering the destruction of the stamps after they were counted, with the exact number required for closing the accounting records.
Following the verification mandated by the resolution, the Ministry of Finance confirmed the receipt of the stamps through letter no. 1859 dated March 10, 1859, signed by Minister Eugen Alcaz. The same document also bears the signature of controller Ion Bogdan, dated March 13, 1859, with the number 1286.
According to the Philatelic Almanac of 1985, no further information exists in the archives about these stamps after this date.
