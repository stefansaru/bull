## 🖋 The Relay Houses and Their Seals, 1822–1834

The oldest devices belong to the **relay-post houses** — the institution described
at [[../../entities/concepts/menzil-relay-system|the menzil relay post]], leased
across this period to the Greek contractor **C. Scufa**. In Moldavia the *Casa
Menzilului Moldovei* struck the earliest Romanian postal marking Dragomir
catalogues: [[../../entities/handstamps/SM22|**SM22**]] (1822), known from a **single
surviving impression** on a postal order of 7 September 1825 to the Dorohoi post —
issued under *"trienalul antreprizei Scufa, 1822/25"*, the lease made tangible. As
the three-year terms rolled over, new dies followed:
[[../../entities/handstamps/SM27|**SM27**]] (1827, a double circle showing a
postillion sounding a horn between two trees) and
[[../../entities/handstamps/SM30|**SM30**]] (1830). Each bears, in Cyrillic
majuscules, the same title of the house: *"PECETEA KĂSII MENZILULUI MOLDAVIEI"*.

Wallachia kept its own central office, the *Casa Menzilurilor Valahiei*, whose seal
[[../../entities/handstamps/SV27|**SV27**]] (1827) shows not a bull's head but the
**cross-bearing eagle in flight** above the post-cart and its galloping
postillion — the emblem of Țara Românească — under the legend *"PECETE KĂSII
MENZILURILOR WALACHII 1827"*. The two provinces, two houses, two emblems: the whole
pre-stamp record runs in this parallel.
