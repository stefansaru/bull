## ⨀ C1 ⨀ C1a Frame size

<!-- u:u00878 kind:quad -->
There are differences between the measurements published by Kiriac in the 1974 catalogue, in Ștampilografie poștală (1990), and those recorded by Heimbüchler (1994). The table below shows the measurements documented by Heimbüchler. While Kiriac (1990) stated that the earliest date is 1857-11-10, Heimbüchler suggests—based on an existing cover dated 1857-10-10—that the C1 was used even earlier.
Because the handstamps were engraved manually, variations occur in the width, height, and inclination of different strikes. In cases of overinking or underinking, the measured dimensions may differ by up to approximately 0.4 mm. 
The ink color also varies, although it is very likely the same as that used for the M1 handstamps, including the short-period red ink applied at Jassy. Nevertheless, a clearly struck handstamp can still be attributed to a unique post office.
