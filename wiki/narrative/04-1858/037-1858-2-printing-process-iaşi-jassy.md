## 1858 📅 🐃 2: Printing process 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00670 kind:quad -->
Printing process
The stamps from the second issue were all printed between 1858 and 1862, using the same technique as the first issue. The Stamp Workshop was moved from Iaşi to Bucharest in december 1861, and the 5 Parale stamps Type II from 1862 were printed in Bucharest. The 5 Parale Type I were printed in Iaşi, together with the 40 and 80 Parale.
