## 1858 📅 📯 Decision for single postal tariff 🌍 Moldavia

<!-- u:u00659 kind:quad -->
Decision for single postal tariff
The implementation of the postal tariff from February 14, 1857 (J), based on distance and weight, proved difficult to calculate and unsatisfactory to the public, who generally did not consider distance.
