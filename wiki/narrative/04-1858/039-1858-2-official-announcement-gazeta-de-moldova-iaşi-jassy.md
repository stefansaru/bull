## 1858 📅 🐃 2: Official announcement 📰 Gazeta de Moldova 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00676 kind:text -->
1858-10-30    J📅G    1858-11-11

<!-- u:u00677 kind:quad -->
Official announcement
On October 30, 1858, Gazeta de Moldova announced that, starting November 1, postage for letters within the Principality would be standardized at one leu for any distance, with registered letters requiring two lei. 
Stamps valued at one leu and two lei would be issued and available for sale. At that time, 1 leu was equivalent to 40 parale, although the one leu coin did not yet exist in 1858. 
The weight limit for this rate was 2.5 dramuri (about 8.07 grams or 0.28 ounces).
