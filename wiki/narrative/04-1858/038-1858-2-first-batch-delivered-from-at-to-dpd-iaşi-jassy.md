## 1858 📅 🐃 2: First batch delivered from A.T. to D.P.D. 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

First batch delivered
The Stamp Workshop delivers the first batch of stamps to the Ministry of Finance on October 27 (J). On the same day, the M.F. forwards the stamps to D.P.D. (M.F. doc. no. 10283).
