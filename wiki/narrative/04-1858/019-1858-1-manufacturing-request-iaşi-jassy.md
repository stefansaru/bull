## 1858 📅 🐃 1: Manufacturing Request 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00529 kind:text -->
1858-05-01    J📅G    1858-05-13

<!-- u:u00530 kind:quad -->
Manufacturing Request
The Moldavian Post (D.P.D.) issues request #975 to the Ministry of Finance (M.F.) for manufacturing the "CAP DE BOUR" postage stamps. These were to be manufactured in accordance with the „INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE TRANSPORTATION OF LETTERS” and the first 3 values are mentioned: 27, 54 and 81 Parale. Agreement concerning a fourth value, 108 Parale, was reached at a later time.
