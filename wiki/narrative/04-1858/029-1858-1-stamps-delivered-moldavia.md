## 1858 📅 🐃 1: Stamps delivered 🌍 Moldavia

<!-- u:u00642 kind:text -->
July 📅 September

<!-- u:u00643 kind:quad -->
Stamps delivered
The stamps were distributed from the central cashier to all dispatch offices in whole sheets (x32), to be cut at their destination. 
Deliveries were made at different times from July until September, but certain locations – Cahul, Bolgrad, Ismail (cities that emerged after the Crimean War) and Huși – did not receive stamps. 
In the case of Huși, delivery was suspended due to an investigation into fraud involving the sender Gh. Bălănescu and the district treasurer Gh. Mîrza, who also held the position of head of the dispatch office.
