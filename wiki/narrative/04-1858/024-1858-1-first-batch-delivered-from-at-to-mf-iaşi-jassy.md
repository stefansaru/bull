## 1858 📅 🐃 1: First batch delivered from A.T. to M.F. 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00623 kind:text -->
1858-07-11    J📅G    1858-07-23

<!-- u:u00624 kind:quad -->
First batch delivered to M.F.
The Stamp Workshop deposits the first instalment of stamps in the treasury of the Ministry of Finance, with the intention of putting them into circulation from 15 July 1858 (J).
According to A.T. document no. 62 of 11 July 1858 (J), 5984 stamps in 187 sheets were deposited, as shown in the following table.
