## 1858 📅 🐃 1: Withdraw from circulation 🌍 Iaşi (Jassy)

<!-- u:u00679 kind:text -->
1858-11-01    J📅G    1858-11-13

<!-- u:u00680 kind:quad -->
Withdraw from circulation
As of November 1, 1858, the "AUROCHS HEAD" stamps of the first issue were withdrawn from circulation. Of the stamps printed and delivered in two batches (24`064), 11`746 were sold and 12`318 remained in stock. These were in circulation for 102 days in the capital, Iasi, and 89 days in the district capitals, except for Galati, where they circulated for 61 days.
