## 🐃 Nibelungenlied

<!-- u:u00126 kind:text -->
📅 XII – XIIIth centuries

<!-- u:u00127 kind:quad -->
🐃 Song of the Nibelungs
In the epic Germanic poem „Nibelungenlied”, the character Siegfried is noted for his incredible feat of strength when he hunts and kills four aurochs in the forest, showcasing his heroic attributes.
„Then there fell by his hand a buffalo, an elk, four grim aurochs, and a bear, nor could deer or hind escape him, so swift and wight was he.”
