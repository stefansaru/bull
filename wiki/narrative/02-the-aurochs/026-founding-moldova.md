🐃 Founding Moldova
The aurochs hunt symbolized Dragoş' dominion over the wild landscape, further cementing his role as the founder of Moldova. Overcome by loyalty to his fallen companion, Dragoş named the river Moldova after his dog, and the land around it took on the same name, marking the birth of a new principality. He decided that the symbol of Moldova will be an aurochs head. The hunt that led Dragoş to Moldova is legendary, depicting him chasing an aurochs across the Carpathians from Maramureş to the Moldova River, where the animal was ultimately slain. This event is celebrated in the lore as his "dismounting" (descălecat), marking the beginning of Moldova as a geopolitical entity. It is important to note that while these narratives are rich in detail and imbued with national pride, they carry the characteristics of myth rather than verified historical events. The accounts vary, and no contemporary documents conclusively pinpoint the exact circumstances of Moldova's founding. Therefore, these stories should be appreciated as part of Moldova's cultural heritage and historical mythology rather than as precise historical accounts.
