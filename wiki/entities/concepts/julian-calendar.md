# 📅 Julian calendar (J, Old Style)

The Julian calendar was the civil and church calendar of Moldavia and Wallachia
when the Bull's Head stamps were issued. Official documents, postal orders and
newspapers of 1858 are dated in it, so a date found in a source of the period is
**Julian unless stated otherwise** (convention C-04).

The Julian year overestimates the solar year, so its dates drift about one day
every 128 years against the Gregorian calendar. By 1859 the gap was **12 days**: a
Wednesday was a Wednesday in both calendars, but 1 June (J) was 13 June (G)
([[../../narrative/21-calendar/001-calendar|Calendar]]). The gap grew to 13 days
from March 1900 until Romania changed calendars in 1919.
