# FM / FV — the franco family and the sigillary handstamps of the leased road-post

Dragomir's *Ștampilografie* opens its **`FM` / `FV`** chapter (§1.3.1 *FV Valahia*,
§1.3.2 *FM Moldova*) as a family of pre-stamp devices distinct from the office
**seals** (`SM`/`SV`) and the town **pre-philatelic** handstamps (`PM`/`PV`). What
the chapter actually catalogues are the **sigillary handstamps struck on the printed
transport forms** (*formulare de transport*) of the **leased road-post** of the two
principalities — the paperwork by which the post moved travellers, couriers and mail
"*din poștă în poștă*" (from stage to stage)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 44, 57).
This page describes that family and how it relates to the two other things the word
**franco** names in this wiki: the FRANCO/PORTO prepayment/accounting handstamps and
the 1858 documentary apparatus.

> **A naming caution.** The reference key expands the composite codes as **`FM` =
> *Franco Moldavia*** and **`FV` = *Franco Valahia***
> ([[../references/books/dragomir-stampilografie-1990#indicative-codes-marking-classes|code key]]),
> by analogy with the class prefixes. But Dragomir's §1.3 text and figures show the
> chapter's true subject to be the **seals on road-post transport forms**, not
> "FRANCO"-lettered prepayment marks. The letter **F**'s expansion is **not spelled
> out** in the pages read here; treat *"Franco Moldavia / Valahia"* as the code
> gloss, and the **transport-form / road-post seal** as the documented content
> "⚠ (OCR — verify against a clean copy of §1.3)".

## What the FV / FM devices are

The Wallachian forms (`FV`) are **large-format printed sheets, framed with
ornamental arabesque borders** that Dragomir numbers **Md. I – Md. XIII** (his
summary sentence reads *Md. I – Md. XI*, but the individual form captions run to
**Md. XIII** — a slip in the source, [[../../meta/errata|errata]]). In the
upper field of the seal **predominates the crowned, cross-bearing eagle (*acvila
cruciată*, the "vulture" of philatelic usage) in full flight, head turned to its
right, holding in its talons the sword and the mace — the emblems of power**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 44).

The seals themselves are **round (oval by exception), single-circle (sometimes
tightly and finely doubled) or double-circle, of the sigillary type, occasionally
cut in the negative**. Their image shows the **post cart drawn by four horses driven
by a postillion (*surugiu*) mounted on the near wheel-horse and in the cart (*olac*)** —
facing right ("*corect*") or reversed to the left ("*conturnat*") — with or without
the attributes of sovereignty in the talons; **below the line of the road runs the
year, or a postal horn**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 44).

These road-administration seals (of the *Direcția / Despărțirea / Casa Poștelor* of
each road) are distinct from the individual **town post-station seals** reading "ПОШТА
[town] АНУЛ [year]" (Slatina 1851, Rîmnicu Vîlcii 1856, Tîrgoviște 1851, Cîmpulung 1858
…) with a mounted courier in the field, which Dragomir catalogues separately in his
station-seal plate (`Planșă 7`) — see [[../handstamps/SV-stations|SV-stations]].

## Moldavia (`FM`) — mostly manuscript

The Moldavian post is the opposite case. **"Poșta Moldovei a utilizat cu precădere
«miziluri» manuscrise … și foarte rar formulare tipărite"** — Moldavia used
**handwritten *miziluri*** predominantly and printed forms only very rarely
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 57). Dragomir
gives **one example of an "FM" from 1845**, extracting from its text: *"L-lor căpitani
de pe drumul mare veți slobozi din stanție în stanție cîte o căruță cu patru cai și
surugiu … în mergere la Eși [Iași] și înapoi"* — the road captains were to release, from
stage to stage, a cart with four horses and a postillion, running to Iași and back. The
figure is a **"Formular tip «FM» al Casei Poștelor Moldovei"**, its seal legend reading
**"КАСА ПОШТИЛОР МОЛДОВЕЙ · Anul 1845"** (Col. L. N. Cristea)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 121, photo 57).
See [[../handstamps/FM|FM]].

## The lease periods that date the forms

The Wallachian forms are organised by the **leases of the road-post** (the year in the
seal is the lease/matrix year; the surviving form is usually later)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 44–47):

- **Trienalul I (1830–1832)** — first three-year lease, sold to the contractor
  **Hristofor Sachelarie, 1 Aug 1830** (3,228 horses; a cart and a postillion per
  relay; the road captain kept a stitched register of travellers and horses).
- **Trienalul II (1833–1836)** — the **first known transport form** survives from it:
  Poșta Cerneți **No. 93 of 27 June 1836** (170 × 153 mm, frame Md. I), releasing six
  horses for Ghiță Bucșănescu to Craiova, signed V. Zorileanu (Fr. Heimbüchler coll.).
- **Cincinalele (five-year leases): 1836–1840, 1841–1845, 1846–1850, 1851–1855**, and
  the **extended cincinal from 1856**, each re-let to several road contractors by road
  (Focșani, Brăila, Călărași, Giurgiu, Craiova, Cîineni, Tîrgoviște, Ploiești …).

The per-device catalogue (FV33 … FV60, with the offices *Direcția / Despărțirea /
Casa Poștelor* of each road) is tabulated at [[../handstamps/FV|FV]].

## "Franco" here versus the other franco/porto

Three different things in this wiki carry the word *franco*, and they should not be
conflated:

1. **This family (`FM`/`FV`)** — the pre-stamp **sigillary handstamps on road-post
   transport forms**, catalogued by Dragomir. Emblem: the Wallachian **eagle** (`FV`);
   Moldavian forms are rare (`FM`).
2. **The FRANCO / PORTO prepayment handstamps** — the linear town handstamps *FRANCO
   [town]* / *PORTO [town]* that marked whether a letter was **paid at departure
   (franco)** or **payable on arrival (porto, postage due)**, and the *Franco* / *Porto*
   accounting columns of the money- and parcel-forwarding forms. These belong to the
   1858 reformed post and are described in **[[franco-porto-apparatus-1858]]** — not
   duplicated here.
3. **The 1858 documentary apparatus** — the registers (*Jurnalul scrisorilor
   franco-porto*), *facturi*, *recipise* and reports through which every letter was
   taxed and accounted, also at **[[franco-porto-apparatus-1858]]**.

The connecting thread is prepayment and accounting: the road-post transport forms of
the `FM`/`FV` family are the **pre-reform** stratum of that same institutional
machinery — the leased carriage of mail and passengers that the 1858 state post, with
its franco/porto columns and adhesive stamps, would replace.
