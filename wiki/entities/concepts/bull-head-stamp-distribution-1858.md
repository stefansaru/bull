# Distributing the 1858 Bull's Heads — Directorate, bureaus and "outside expeditors"

The [[../issues/issue-bull-1|first *Cap de bou* issue]] page records *what* the four
1858 stamps were; this page follows *where they physically went* once printed. The
archival reports transcribed by Wertheimer-Ghika let us trace the whole chain — from
the **Direcția Poștelor** in Iași, down through the district (*ținut*) bureaus, and out
to a fringe of small "outside expeditors" at passenger post-stations — and they let us
watch bureaus receive, hold, sell, reconcile and **return** their stock month by month.
Because the issue lived only from July to October 1858, that paper trail is also the
best contemporary explanation for why genuine used Bull's Heads are so few (see
[[scarcity-of-bull-heads]]). For the reform that created the Directorate, see
[[postal-history-romania]].

## The supply chain

The stamps flowed down a fixed hierarchy. At the top, the **Ministry of Finance**
placed each order with the [[../places/atelia-timbrului|Atelia Timbrului]] (stamp
workshop): the Ministry "sends with each order" the commission for printing, and the
workshop, "after executing the commission, returns them to the Mi[nistry]"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 65). From
the Ministry the stock passed to the **Direcția Poștelor**, which distributed it to the
district bureaus. Below the bureaus sat a third tier: the *sameș* (district cashier) of
a *ținut* "sent the stamps received from the Directorate of Posts to the Focșani postal
bureau, which in turn sent a small stock of these stamps to the **outside expeditors**"
(*expeditorilor din afară*) ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika
1944]] photo 40).

Those "outside expeditors" were not full bureaus. Wertheimer-Ghika calls them
**auxiliary offices** — letter-forwarding points at passenger post-stations — and ties
them to Ministry of Finance order No. 995 (*"Condițiile Poștelor"*), which laid down
"that the **captains of the passenger post-stations will also serve as postal
expeditors**" ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo
40). The Focșani report proves this was real, not theoretical: the Focșani office "sent
to the post-stations within its range postal stamps, to be used by them in forwarding
the private letters" handed in there — the named example being the **Adjud** station,
which had received its own postmark already in 1858
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 40). A similar
selling point existed at **Darabani**, on the Russian frontier in Dorohoi district,
"having an office for the sale of stamped sheets since 1856"; the 1858 Ministry of
Finance order was precisely "to distribute to these bureaus postal stamps for sale"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 31).

## What was ordered — split "for the Directorate and for outside"

The very first order already distinguished a central reserve from a provincial one.
The Directorate's report asks for stamps "**both for the Directorate and for outside**"
(*atât pentru direcție și pentru afară*): "10,000 stamps … for the Directorate and for
outside"; "6,000 stamps, for the Directorate 3,000 and for outside another 3,000"; and
"1,000 stamps … for the Directorate and for outside 1,000," the 108-parale value being
tied to *scrisori cu retepis* (registered/return-receipt letters)
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 11). A
marginal note adds that "the figure of **1,000 stamps at 27 parale was added**" to the
run ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 11).

The document then gives a total: "there were printed, or **exactly 24,064 stamps**,"
across the four values, in two batches dated **11 July** and **21 July** 1858
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 11). The
per-value column is badly scrambled in the OCR, but its legible figures — **6,016**
(27 parale), **10,016** (54 parale), **2,016** (81 parale) and **6,016** (108 parale) —
sum to 24,064 and match the totals the secondary literature repeats
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 11; cf.
[[scarcity-of-bull-heads]]). ⚠️ Treat the **batch subtotals** (numbers such as 992, 480,
3520, 5024, 9024, 1536, 2496) as `unverified` — that part of the table is mis-ordered in
the scan.

## Where the stamps were sold

Distribution reached well beyond the district capitals. Photo 11 lists the selling
localities from the *Manualul Administrativ al Moldovei* of 1856, "which also fixes the
number of selling offices in each locality," and classes each as a **stație de poște**
(post-station), **birou poștal** (postal bureau) or **expedițiune poștală** (postal
expedition) ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo
11). The places named include **Focșani, Tecuci, Fălticeni, Botoșani, Bacău, Roman,
Piatra, Galați, Huși, Bârlad, Hârlău, Vaslui, Neamțu, Iași, Ocna, Darabani,
Podul-Iloaei, Ștefănești, Burdujeni, Fălciu, Mihăileni, Herța, Sulița, Rădăuți,
Podu-Turcului, Nicorești, Odobești, Panciu** and **Sculeni**
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 11). The
district capitals proper — Dorohoi (with Mihăileni on the frontier), Fălticeni,
Botoșani, Focșani, Tecuci, Roman, Vaslui, Bârlad, Galați, Bacău, Huși, Piatra and Iași —
head the tables of receiving bureaus
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 18).

## The Focșani list — the model document

The single richest record is the **Focșani** monthly return, dated **26 September
1858**, which Wertheimer-Ghika reproduces as a *LISTA* under the sameș's signature. Its
column headings set out the whole accounting cycle: **"Sum received from the Directorate
in the month of August"** (*Suma priimită de la direcție în luna August*); **"Sum used
in the course of August"** (*Suma întrebuințată în cursul lunii August*); **"Sum
remaining as of 1 September"** (*Suma rămasă față către 1-iu Septemvrie*); and a column
for what **"was sent to the outside expeditors"** (*s-au trimis expeditorilor din
afară*) ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 40).
The four rows are the four values, 27 / 54 / 81 / 108 parale. ⚠️ The **numeric cells** of
this table (e.g. 224, 160, 8873, and the para-sums 456, 324, 561, 604, 105, 504, 309)
are so garbled and intermixed with money totals in the OCR that **no per-value quantity
can be quoted with confidence** — flag every figure in the Focșani grid as `unverified`.
What survives cleanly is the *structure*: received → sold → held → passed down.

## Reconciliation: sold, returned, and only two months to do it

A second bureau report reconciles sales against stock. It states plainly that "the
'*Cap de Bou*' stamps of 27, 54, 81 and 108 parale, first issue, **circulated only 2
months!**" ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 62,
p. 116). Within that window the bureau accounts for stamps "**sold from 1 September to
1 October**, entered through the September account," and again "sold from 10 October to
…," with a running total the clerk spells out in words — "**2419 — that is, two thousand
four hundred and nineteen**" — the money handed to the treasury
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 62, p. 116).
⚠️ The money columns beside it (*Lei parale* 1954,32 / 464,8) are `unverified` OCR.
Month-end reconciliation of **sold versus unsold-on-hand** was the standing routine:
a later end-of-month account at Galați tallies stamps "**found on hand, unsold**"
(*găsit față, nevândute*) value by value before they are carried forward
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 62, p. 117).

Wertheimer-Ghika draws the blunt administrative moral himself: **"the distribution of
stamps was not very well organised,"** with the bureaus "remaining from time to time
without stamps" ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo
62, pp. 116–117) — a chronic shortage confirmed by the stream of bureau letters begging
the Directorate to send more (Bacău, Roman, Huși, Tecuci, Galați, Fălticeni), and by the
frequent expedient of marking a letter's tariff in pencil, in cash, "in place of the
postage stamps" when none were to hand
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 70). A short
sale, a thin distribution and a two-month life together explain why so little genuine
used material exists — the primary-document counterpart to the census figures gathered on
[[scarcity-of-bull-heads]].

## Staff and salaries (incidental)

The same establishment papers fix, in passing, who ran this apparatus — figures that also
feed the postal-salaries topic. At the diligence directorate an **epistat** (supervisor)
guarding the coach stock drew **140 lei** a month, and a stable-hand for washing the
coaches **100 lei**, "these three reckoned from 1 May"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 18). Item "K"
provides **four expeditors — for Ismail, Bolgrad, Cahul and Chilia (or Reni) — at 200 lei
a month each**, "not foreseen in the budget of the postal-establishment project …
reckoned from 1 May" ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]]
photo 19). And the *United Principalities* budget project of 1860, quoted to prove the
outside-expeditor tier existed, provides "**indemnities granted to the postmasters,
charged with being warehousemen and expeditors — number of agents 40, monthly salary 100
lei**" ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 40).
