# Bull's Head postmarks in the postal archives (Wertheimer-Ghika 1944)

> **What this page adds.** The [[../postmarks/census|auction census]] and [[../postmarks/kiriac-figures|Kiriac figure catalogue]] record the *devices* — round MOLDOVA datestamps, framed FRANCO handstamps, PORTO / RECEPISSE marks — as they survive on stamps and covers. This page ties those devices to **towns through the surviving Directorate-of-Posts paperwork** transcribed and reproduced by Wertheimer-Ghika (1944): which bureau's marking is *named in a document*, when it was created, and what it was for. It is a document-grounded, town-by-town evidence list. Cross-reference, do not restate, the census/Kiriac rows.

> ⚠ **OCR caveat (DR-006).** The sole source is a noisy Romanian OCR of photographed pages; town names appear in period German/French transliterations *and* are mangled by OCR (e.g. `FORSCHAY`, `FOKSCHAMIS` = Focșani; `DAROEHOI`/`DOROEHOI` = Dorohoi; `BOTOSCHENI`/`Botuschani` = Botoșani; `TEKOUTCI` = Tecuci; `HUCHII` = Huși; `NIAMTZU` = Târgu Neamț). Correct spellings and diacritics (DR-011) are restored below; the original OCR token is given in parentheses so the reading can be checked against the photo. Readings I could **not** confidently reconstruct are flagged **⚑**.

## The two marking families the documents name

The archive distinguishes two purposes, stated verbatim: *"Cele cu «Porto» pentru trimiteri platibile la sosire. Cele cu «Franco» serveau pentru trimiterile platite la plecare"* — **PORTO** marks for items payable on arrival, **FRANCO** marks for items prepaid at departure ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 37, p. 99). Alongside these run the round **datestamps** ("ștampile rotunde cu data zilei") and the frameless **RECEPISSE** registered-mail marks catalogued as [[../postmarks/kiriac-figures|Kiriac fig. 40–54 and 55]].

Two dated introductions are documented:
- The money-and-parcel **PORTO / FRANCO** handstamps ("Ștampile pentru trimiteri de bani și pachete") were created at *"începutul lunei Septembrie"* — early September **1858** ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 37, p. 99).
- The **framed FRANCO** handstamps of [[../postmarks/kiriac-figures|Kiriac fig. 57–74]] (the census's `FRANCO / …` rows, *Ștampilografie* fig. 301–319) were *"introduse în toamna 1859"* — autumn **1859**, illustrated *"pe mărci de 40 și 80 Parale"* (i.e. cancelling the **second Bull's Head issue**) ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 85, p. 162). This corroborates the census's placement of `FRANCO / JASSY`, `FRANCO / GALATZ`, `FRANCO / ISMAILU`, `FRANCO / ROMAN`, `FRANCO / FOKSCHANY`, `FRANCO / KAHUL`, `FRANCO / BERLAD`, `FRANCO / BACEU` under Issue II.

## How this corroborates and extends the wiki's postmark records

- **Dates the marking families.** The [[../postmarks/kiriac-figures|Kiriac catalogue]] dates the framed FRANCO group to "1859 – iunie 1865" from the devices; the archive independently fixes **early September 1858** for the PORTO/FRANCO *money-and-parcel* handstamps and **autumn 1859** for the framed FRANCO on 40- and 80-parale stamps ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photos 37, 85). This aligns the census's `FRANCO / …` rows (all Issue II) with a documentary introduction date.
- **PORTO on covers, PORTO in the ledger.** The census records `ROMAN / PORTO` (fig. 292/1490); the archive shows PORTO was a *system* — a whole `Jurnalul porto scrisorilor` (photo 54) and money/parcel PORTO handstamps at Botoșani, Tecuci and Galați — not an isolated mark.
- **Confirms bureaus the census only sees as datestamps.** Botoșani, Tecuci, Bacău, Dorohoi, Mihăileni, Ismail, Roman, Huși, Cahul, Târgu Neamț and Târgu Ocna all appear in the census's Kiriac-40–54 datestamp list; the documents add *when* and *why* their FRANCO/PORTO handstamps existed.
- **Extends with attestations the census lacks.** **Darabani** (rare Cyrillic *Târgulu Darabanii* office stamp), **Fălticeni's** Cyrillic *Franko*, and the **1858 Ismail office seals** are documentary markings not present as auction lots in [[../postmarks/census|census]] — genuine additions to the town-by-town record.
- **The "orthography of the stamps."** W-G reproduces a Directorate table of bureau opening dates whose town spellings are, verbatim, *"ortografia … a ștampilelor timpului"* — the German transliterations (Bakeu, Botuschani, Fokschani, Foltitscheni, Husch, Jassy, Tekutsch, Waslui …) that the census notes as the catalogues' period spellings, here sourced to the offices' own files ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 46).
