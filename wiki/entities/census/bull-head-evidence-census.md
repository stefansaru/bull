## Method & caveats

- **Auction layer** (`auc-*` ids) is the mechanical extraction from the auction catalogues (`tools/census.py`): lot- and postmark-level records. Verify against the source page before citing a specific claim.
- **Mined layer** is hand-read from the narrative/document sources by source (each row cites a photo/page). Diacritics restored on quotation (DR-011).
- One physical item may appear as several rows (e.g. a cover *and* its postmark). Deduplication is deliberately light — each row is a distinct *documented mention*.
- `?` issue = denomination not stated in the source.
