# Cârjan & Manole — *Catalogul timbrelor poștale românești*, vol. I: 1858–1947 (2006)

A modern (2006) Romanian stamp catalogue by **Lazăr Cârjan** and **Mircea Gheorghe
Manole**, published by **Enciclopedia RAO**, Bucharest (ISBN 978-973-717-073-6;
scientific consultants **Leonard Pușcanu** and **Silviu Dragomir**)
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 2). Vol. I
covers **1858–1947**. For the Bull's Head it is the most **design-analytic** of the
three catalogues mined here: alongside prices it gives per-value **plating
diagnostics** (numbered die characteristics, Fig. 48–57), circulation/tirage data,
notable cancellations and composite frankings, the 1891 **Gorjan reprints** and the
**Moroiu forgeries**.

Terminology note: this catalogue calls the emblem **"Cap de zimbru"** (aurochs/bison
head) rather than *Cap de bour*
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 3). See
[[../concepts/aurochs-symbolism]].

## What it covers (Bull's Head)

- **Emisiunea I** — 1858 (dated here **15 July – 31 October**; note the other two
  catalogues give **22 July**), Moldova, *Porto Scrisori* in Cyrillic letters,
  colour-printed on coloured paper, single engraved die-seal per value, imperforate;
  four values 27 / 54 / 81 / 108 parale
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 12).
- **Emisiunea a II-a** — 1858 (1 Nov 1858 – 1 May 1862), bull's head + post-horn in a
  round-cornered rectangle, Latin-letter *Porto Scrisorei* / *Porto Gazetei*, value
  "PAR" in Cyrillic; three values 5 / 40 / 80 parale
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 16).

## Numbering system

Catalogue numbers carry the prefix **`L.P.Z.C.`** (the catalogue's own list code for
the *Cap de Zimbru* issues):

- Issue I = **L.P.Z.C. 1–4** (1 = 27 P, 2 = 54 P, 3 = 81 P, 4 = 108 P)
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 12).
- Issue II = **L.P.Z.C. 5–7** (5 = 5 P, 6 = 40 P, 7 = 80 P), with cadru-întreg /
  cadru-spart split (**5B / 5A**) and shade sub-variants (e.g. **6Aa–6Ce**,
  **7Aa–7Cd**)
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 16, 18, 20).

**Valuations are given in EURO** (2006), in columns **`*`** (unused), **`⊙`** (used)
and, in the variant tables, **`△`** (on fragment) and **`✉`** (on cover); fine
square-margined copies take **+150–200 %** over the base cote
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 12). Each value
also has a **Coeficienți de evaluare** table (multipliers for pairs, sheet-margin
pairs, blocks of 4, tête-bêche, on-newspaper)
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 16, 19).

### Bull's Head cotes (EUR, base) — as read from the photos

Issue I (L.P.Z.C. 1–4), columns unused `*` / used `⊙` / on-cover `✉`
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 12):

| № | Denom. | unused * | used ⊙ | cover ✉ |
|---|--------|---------:|-------:|--------:|
| 1 | 27 P negru / roz pal | 38 000 | 17 000 | 55 000 |
| 2 | 54 P albastru / verzui | 20 000 | 8 000 | 30 000 |
| 3 | 81 P albastru / albăstrui | 40 000 | 45 000 | 60 000 |
| 4 | 108 P albastru / roz | 27 000 | 16 000 | 28 000 |

Issue II (L.P.Z.C. 5–7), base cote unused `*` / used `⊙`, from the dedicated pages
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 16, 18, 20):

| № | Denom. | unused * | used ⊙ |
|---|--------|---------:|-------:|
| 5 | 5 P negru, h. albă, cadru întreg (1858) | 25 000 | 18 000 |
| 5A | 5 P cadru spart (1862) | 600 | — |
| 6 | 40 P albastru, h. albă (1859) | 450 | 350 |
| 7 | 80 P cărămiziu, h. albă (1858) | 850 | 700 |

> ⚠ The issue-II **summary table** (photo 16) prints **40 P = 400 / 300** and
> **80 P = 1 675 / 800**, which disagree with the dedicated-page base cotes above
> (450 / 350 and 850 / 700). Treated here as an internal inconsistency; the
> dedicated pages are taken as authoritative
> ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 16, 18, 20).
> The rarest sub-variants dwarf the base: 5 P on **blue paper** 35 000 / 22 000
> (photo 16), 80 P on **azure paper (1861)** 10 500–11 500 unused (photo 20).

### Tirages (both catalogues agree)

27 P 6 016 (sold 3 691); 54 P 10 016 (sold 4 772); 81 P 2 016 (sold 709);
108 P 6 016 (sold 2 574) — total 11 746 for issue I
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 4, 12).
Issue II: 5 P 7 008 (blue) + 16 032 (white/yellow); 40 P 106 032; 80 P 60 072
([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 16, 18, 20).

## What it uniquely offers

- **Plating / die-characteristic analysis** per value (numbered diagnostic points on
  legend letters, horns, ears, star, post-horn; Fig. 48–57)
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 12–22).
- **Shade tables** with EUR cotes across `*`/`⊙`/`△`/`✉`, plus evaluation
  coefficients for multiples and tête-bêche.
- Notable **cancellations and composite frankings** recorded per value (e.g. 81 P
  with early BAKEU-MOLDOVA 4/8 / late GALATZ 17/10; a single 108+108+81+10 cover)
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photo 12).
- Sections on the **Gorjan reprints (1891)** and **Moroiu forgeries (1858)**
  ([[../../sources/carjan-manole-timbre-2006/text|Cârjan-Manole]] photos 4, 6).
