# Wertheimer-Ghika 1944 — *Documentele Poștei Române: Cartea mărcilor «Cap de bou»*

A Romanian-language monograph that argues the case for the genuine 1858 Moldavian
*Cap de bou* stamps and their postmarks **directly from official postal-archive
documents**, most of them here transcribed and published for the first time
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|Wertheimer-Ghika 1944]] photo 6, "*primele documente autentice ale poștelor pe care voiu clădi adevărata istorie a primelor noastre mărci*").
It is the documentary foundation of modern Bull's Head study and a landmark of
[[../concepts/early-philatelic-study|early philatelic scholarship]] on Romania.

## Author and publication

*Documentele Poștei Române: Cartea mărcilor «Cap de bou»* by **Jacques
Wertheimer-Ghika**, published by **Editura Cartea Românească, București** in
**1944** ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 1–2). The
author writes from his own collection: the illustrated pieces and many of the
archival documents "*fac parte din colecția autorului*" and so, he stresses,
"*sunt deci ușor de controlat*" (are easy to verify)
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 7). The Preface is
dated **February 1944** ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]]
photo 7).

## Purpose and structure

The title-page plan announces that *Documentele Poștei Române*, drawn "*după
documente inedite*" (from unpublished documents), comprises five parts:
**I. Cartea mărcilor «Cap de bou»; II. Cartea mărcilor «Principatele Unite»;
III. Poșta pasagerilor; IV. Începuturile telegrafului român; V. Poșta străină în
Principate** ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 4). The
present volume opens with a **Preface** (photo 5–7) and an **Introduction**
(photo 8–9), then unrolls a large corpus of transcribed primary sources —
**rapoarte** (reports), **referate**, **scrisori**, **chitanțe**, **recipise**,
the **blanchet de poștă**, and the day-to-day registers — reproduced almost
verbatim in their "*formă și stil naiv, chiar primitiv*," in the old Cyrillic
hand and often without punctuation
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 7). Series of costs
and inventories are set out under headings **LITERA A / B / C**
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 23–24), and the
mechanics of dispatch are shown through the **«Jurnalul scrisorilor
franco-porto»**, a franking-and-postage-due register reproduced in facsimile
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 50). Later
sections carry the promised material on **passenger post**, the **beginnings of
the Romanian telegraph** — using the *Dare de Seamă a Direcțiunii Generale a
Telegrafelor și Poștelor* for 1872/73
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 46) — and the
**foreign posts** operating on Moldavian soil
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 8).

## Method

The book's method is **evidentiary**: to reconstruct "*starea poștelor din
Moldova pe timpul apariției primelor mărci românești*" strictly from official
reports and files, so that every claim can be checked against a dated document
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 7). From these files
Wertheimer-Ghika establishes the printing of the stamps at the
[[../places/atelia-timbrului|Atelia Timbrului]], their **distribution to the
post offices** in 1858 — the report No. 975 of 1 May 1858 of the Directorate of
Posts ([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 30) and the
tables of quantities printed and shipped
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 11) — and the
genuineness of contested town **postmarks** attested by the same archive, e.g.
the two long-disputed handstamps of Darabani, "*pe nedrept contestate, până la
găsirea ultimelor documente*"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 11). These
threads are developed in the sibling concept pages
[[../concepts/bull-head-stamp-distribution-1858]],
[[../concepts/franco-porto-apparatus-1858]] and
[[../concepts/bull-head-postmarks-from-documents]].

## Polemical stance

The Preface is openly combative — *legend/opinion, expressly framed by the author
as such* (C-17). Wertheimer-Ghika holds that earlier writing on the Moldavian and
United-Principalities stamps was "*rezultatul unei imaginații fecunde... lipsit de
bază documentară*," faulting foreign authors (Brunel, Poncelet, Moens, Becton,
Duerst) for distance from the archive and resident writers (v. Witzleben,
Zoscsak) for publishing without documentary proof
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 5). His sharpest
charge is against **fantasy stamps ("mărci fanteziste")**: Th. C. Aslan's
Academy-crowned *Studiu asupra Monopolurilor în România* (1906) reproduced, at
p.61, forgeries by the convicted falsifier **Moroiu** as if they were Romania's
"*primele și adevăratele noastre mărci poștale, emise în anul 1854*"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 6). He closes by
naming the dealers and society officials he blames for spreading rumours of "*așa
zise ștampile false*," ending "*Șarlatania a murit, trăiască filatelia română!*"
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 6–7). The wider
authenticity debate this book opened is treated at
[[../concepts/bull-head-authentication-and-fantasy-stamps]] (DR-006).

## Significance

By putting the postal archive itself on the page, this work shifted Romanian
philately "*de pe loc*" and gave the study of the Bull's Heads a documentary
spine that catalogues such as [[kiriac-1974|Kiriac 1974]] would later build on. It
remains the primary published witness to the 1858 distribution records, the
franco-porto apparatus, and the authenticity of the early Moldavian postmarks
([[../../sources/wertheimer-ghika-1944/text|W-G 1944]] photo 7).
