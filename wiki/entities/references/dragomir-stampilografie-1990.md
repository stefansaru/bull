# Kiriac Dragomir — *Ștampilografie Poștală (România: 1822–1910)* (1990)

The standard reference for **Romanian postal handstamps and seals** — the devices
that struck the mail rather than the adhesive stamps stuck to it. Written by the
engineer **Kiriac Dragomir**, author of the 1974 *Catalogul mărcilor poștale
românești* ([[kiriac-1974]]), and published by the **Federația Filatelică Română**;
the foreword is by **Dr. L. N. Cristea (Radu Pârvan)** and the postface by F.F.R.
president **Av. Mircea Stănculescu**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|raw OCR]] photos 3, 7). It is
the "second stage" of the systematic study of Romanian markings, following
**Grigore Racoviceanu**'s 1963 *Ștampilele poștale folosite în țările române*
(the 1963 pioneer work) and Dragomir's own 1974 catalogue (photo 3).

Why it matters here: it is the **public taxonomy** the wiki's handstamp pages were
flagged as needing (see [[../handstamps/PM52|PM52]]). Dragomir catalogues each
device under a compact **code** and a **figure/plate** number, covering **both
principalities from 1822** — Moldavia *and* Wallachia (Țara Românească) — through
1910. It is the direct source for the wiki's per-device handstamp entities.

> **Known issues.** The book contains internal inconsistencies (duplicate figure
> numbers, an unexpanded `F` code, a device listed only in a table, etc.). These are
> logged transparently at [[../../meta/errata|meta/errata]] rather than silently
> "corrected".

## The classification system (Dragomir's codes)

Dragomir uses a **decimal classification** of chapters and a two-part code for every
device. A code is built as **`[indicative][year][variant]`** — a type prefix, the
last two digits of the year of issue, and an optional lower-case variant letter
(`SM58a`). Composite prefixes combine a class with a province, so **`PM`** =
*Prefilatelice + Moldova*, **`FV`** = *Franco + Valahia*. Each device also carries a
**`Fig. N`** illustration (reproduced life-size for the classic markings) and is
grouped into a **`Planșă` (plate)**. Two evaluation figures are attached: a
**coeficient** (multiplier for the stamp, by rarity of the marking) and a
**punctaj** (points, for the device on unfranked pieces) — a scheme Dragomir
introduced in his 1974 catalogue (photos 8, 10).

The amprentă (impression) itself is read as **emblemă + legendă** (emblem + legend).
The first Romanian seals are heraldic: **Moldavia's emblem is the *cap de bour*
(bull's head)**, Wallachia's the **eagle** (*acvila* / "vulturul" in philately) —
and Dragomir notes the bull's head "appears for the first time in philately on the
*cap de bour* stamps" of 22 July and 1 November 1858 (photo 10). Legends run
clockwise from the left margin, at first in the **Romanian Cyrillic** alphabet,
evolving to Latin letters. See [[../concepts/moldavia-heraldry]] and
[[../concepts/aurochs-symbolism]].

> A trailing lower-case **`t`** after a symbol marks a **late** device (*ștampile
> târzii*) — e.g. `OBt` = late spa handstamps (photo 8).

**Composite prefixes** used in the catalogue read: **`PM`** = pre-philatelic
Moldavia, **`PV`** = pre-philatelic Wallachia, **`FM`** / **`FV`** = the Moldavian /
Wallachian sections of §1.3. Codes with the same year are distinct devices (e.g.
`SM58` at several bureaus, `SM58a` at the Directorate); disambiguate by
office/description and by the `Fig.` number.

> **`FM`/`FV` caution.** By analogy with the class prefixes these read *Franco
> Moldavia / Valahia*, but the letter **F** is **not spelled out** in Dragomir's §1.3,
> and the section's figures document the **sigillary handstamps on the leased
> road-post's transport forms** (eagle + post-cart), *not* "FRANCO"-lettered
> prepayment marks. The true FRANCO/PORTO prepayment apparatus is
> [[../concepts/franco-porto-apparatus-1858]] and the obliterating FRANCO is `M3`. See
> [[../concepts/franco-porto-handstamps]] "⚠ verify against a clean copy of §1.3".

### Reference apparatus

Claims cite **publications** by sigla — `ASB` Arhivele Statului București, `VM`
Visteria Moldovei (ASB), `MIN` C. Minescu, `CMR` Dragomir's 1974 catalogue, `RAC`
Racoviceanu, `PM` Poșta Moldovei, `F` *Filatelia*, etc. — and **reference
collections** by number, `(col. N)`, from a 29-name list of collectors (Biala,
Cizmaș, Cserni, K. Dragomir, S. Dragomir, … Passalega, Pășcanu, Tripcovici, and the
Munich collector **Fritz Heimbüchler**, frequently cited for the earliest Moldavian
menzil seals) (photo 9, 11).

## Structure of the book

- **Cuvînt înainte** (Cristea/Pârvan) and **Prefață** — a sweep of postal history
  from antiquity (China, Assyria, Egypt) through Dockwra's first "porto" handstamps
  and Renaissance Italy, to the case for studying handstamps (photos 3–7).
- **ABREVIERI** — the classification key above (photos 8–9).
- **Partea I — Ștampilografie Sigilară** (seal handstamps): **`SM`** seals of
  Moldavia (opening with the *Casa Menzilului Moldovei*, 1822–1834 — `SM22`, `SM27`,
  `SM30`) and **`SV`** seals of Wallachia (*Casa Menzilurilor Valahiei* — `SV27`,
  `SV33`, `SV46`…), each with `TABEL` office-tables and `Planșă` plates (photos 10–).
- **Prefilatelice / Filatelice** — the `PM`/`PV` pre-stamp town handstamps, the
  Franco `FM`/`FV`, the quarantine `K`, and the Moldavian obliterators `M1`/`M2`/`M3`
  that cancelled the *Cap de bour* issues, onward to the 1910 post-classic period.
