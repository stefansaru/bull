# Bull's Head — catalogue numbers & valuations across three catalogues

A cross-reference of how three Romanian catalogues **number** and **value** the 1858
Moldavian *Cap de bour* stamps. Sources:
[[spineanu-catalog-marci-1984|Spineanu 1984]] (Lei),
[[carjan-manole-timbre-2006|Cârjan-Manole 2006]] (EUR), and
[[chirices-pascariu-catalog|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] (unit ⚠). **Currencies are not
comparable** — Spineanu quotes 1984 Romanian Lei, Cârjan-Manole quotes 2006 Euros,
and the Chiricheș-Pascariu unit is not legible on the photographed pages (see below).
The value of the table is the **numbering concordance** and the **relative rarity
signal** each catalogue gives.

> **Reading conventions.** `*` = unused, `⊙` = used. All three number the two issues
> identically: **1–4** for issue I (27 / 54 / 81 / 108 parale) and **5–7** for issue
> II (5 / 40 / 80 parale). Cârjan-Manole prefixes the numbers **`L.P.Z.C.`**;
> Spineanu and Chiricheș-Pascariu use plain **`L.P.`**. Every figure below was read
> from the original photos (trusted over the noisy OCR); blanks mean the value is not
> given / not legible in the photographed pages.

## Issue I — 27 / 54 / 81 / 108 parale (base cote, `*` unused / `⊙` used)

| Denom. | Spineanu 1984 (Lei) | Cârjan-Manole 2006 (EUR) | Chiricheș-Pascariu (unit ⚠) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **27 P** | 330 000 / 100 000 (Sp p.3) | 38 000 / 17 000 (C-M p.12) | 61 498.30 / 23 481.20 (Ch-P p.3) |
| **54 P** | 75 000 / 40 000 (Sp p.3) | 20 000 / 8 000 (C-M p.12) | 27 953.80 / 11 181.50 (Ch-P p.3) |
| **81 P** | 260 000 / 280 000 (Sp p.3) | 40 000 / 45 000 (C-M p.12) | 61 498.30 / 67 089.00 (Ch-P p.3) |
| **108 P** | 200 000 / 80 000 (Sp p.3) | 27 000 / 16 000 (C-M p.12) | 33 544.50 / 27 953.80 (Ch-P p.3) |

Cârjan-Manole also gives an **on-cover** column: 27 P 55 000, 54 P 30 000,
81 P 60 000, 108 P 28 000 EUR (C-M p.12). Spineanu's on-cover is **p.a**
(*preț de amator* — on request) for all four (Sp p.3).

**All three agree** the **81 parale is the only value worth more used than unused**
(used ⊙ > unused *), reflecting that only 709 of 2 016 were sold and survivors are
mostly used.

## Issue II — 5 / 40 / 80 parale (representative base line, `*` / `⊙`)

| Denom. | Spineanu 1984 (Lei) | Cârjan-Manole 2006 (EUR) | Chiricheș-Pascariu (unit ⚠) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5 P** full frame, white | 250 000 / 80 000 (Sp p.4) | 25 000 / 18 000 (C-M p.16) | 33 544.50 / 3 354.50 (Ch-P p.5) |
| **5 P** full frame, blue | 330 000 / 100 000 (Sp p.4) | 35 000 / 22 000 (C-M p.16) | 33 544.50 / 20 126.70 (Ch-P p.5) |
| **5 P** broken frame (unissued) | 2 000 / — (Sp p.4) | 600 / — (C-M p.16) | 503.20 / — (Ch-P p.5) |
| **40 P** white/gri (1859) | 2 500 / 1 800 (Sp p.5) | 450 / 350 (C-M p.18) | 464.00 / 464.00 (Ch-P p.5) |
| **40 P** blue paper (1858) | 3 000 / 2 500 (Sp p.5) | — (base = white) | 833.00 / 531.10 (Ch-P p.5) |
| **80 P** white, cărămiziu (1858/59) | 5 500 / 3 000 (Sp p.6) | 850 / 700 (C-M p.20) | 1 498.30 / 475.20 (Ch-P p.5) |
| **80 P** azure paper (1861) | 250 000 / 7 500 (Sp p.6) | 10 500–11 500 / 4 000–5 000 (C-M p.20) | 21 300.80 / 5 758.50 (Ch-P p.5) |

Notes:
- **Cârjan-Manole** headline base cotes are taken from the dedicated per-value pages
  (C-M p.16 for 5 P, p.18 for 40 P, p.20 for 80 P). Its issue-II **summary table**
  (C-M p.16) prints **40 P = 400 / 300** and **80 P = 1 675 / 800**, disagreeing with
  those dedicated pages — an internal inconsistency; dedicated pages taken as
  authoritative. ⚠ (verify).
- **Spineanu** breaks 40 P and 80 P into fine paper/shade tables (6.I/6.II/6.III;
  7.I/7.II/7.III); the lines above are the cheapest base shade on each main paper
  (Sp p.5–6). The **azure-paper 80 P (1861)** is the great mint rarity in all three.
- **Chiricheș-Pascariu** value **unit is not legible** on the photographed pages
  (Ch-P p.3, 5). The figures are recorded verbatim; their proximity to the EUR
  rarities (blue-paper 5 P ≈ 33 500) hints at a converted EUR-like unit, but this is
  **unconfirmed** — do not treat as EUR without checking the catalogue key.

## Where each catalogue is silent / weaker

- **On-cover (`✉`) cotes:** given numerically only by **Cârjan-Manole** (issue I,
  C-M p.12). Spineanu marks covers **p.a**; Chiricheș-Pascariu shows only `*`/`⊙` on
  the photographed pages.
- **International concordance (Yv/Mi/S):** only **Chiricheș-Pascariu** prints it
  (Ch-P p.3, 5).
- **Plating / die diagnostics:** only **Cârjan-Manole** (Fig. 48–57, C-M p.12–22).
- **1858 gold-franc parity:** only **Chiricheș-Pascariu** (Ch-P p.3).
- **Currency comparability:** none — the three use 1984 Lei, 2006 EUR, and an
  unstated unit respectively, so **absolute prices are not directly comparable**;
  only the ordering and rarity structure carry across.

## Cross-checks that agree across catalogues

- **Tirages** (issue I): 27 P 6 016 (sold 3 691), 54 P 10 016 (sold 4 772),
  81 P 2 016 (sold 709), 108 P 6 016 (sold 2 574) — identical in Spineanu (p.3) and
  Cârjan-Manole (p.4, 12); Chiricheș-Pascariu repeats the same figures (p.3).
- **Issue II tirages:** 5 P 7 008 (blue) + 16 032 (white/yellow); 40 P 106 032;
  80 P 60 072 — agree across all three (Sp p.4; C-M p.16; Ch-P p.5).
- **Issue date discrepancy:** Spineanu and Chiricheș-Pascariu date issue I from
  **22 July 1858**; **Cârjan-Manole** gives **15 July 1858** (C-M p.12). ⚠ (noted).
