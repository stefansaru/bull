# Spineanu — *Catalogul mărcilor poștale românești 1858–1947*, vol. I (1984)

The **official 1984 Romanian catalogue**, compiled by **Corneliu Spineanu**,
coordinated by **Angela Harnaj**, with consultants **Nicolae Tripcovici** and
**Mihail Popovici** and scientific referent **Valeriu Neagu**, issued by the
**Direcția Generală a Poștelor și Telecomunicațiilor (DGPTc)**
([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 2). It is the
**earliest** of the three catalogues mined here and the reference for **1984-era Lei
valuations**, detailed **shade / tête-bêche tables**, and the largest-known-multiple
records for the Bull's Head.

Terminology: this catalogue uses **"Cap de bour"** (bull's/ox head)
([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3).

## What it covers (Bull's Head)

- **Emisiunea I** — 1858, **22 July – 31 October**, Moldova, *Porto Scrisori* in
  Cyrillic, colour-printed on coloured paper (horizontally laid for 27/54/108,
  plain for 81), single engraved die per value, imperforate, valid **only within
  Moldavia** ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3).
- **Emisiunea a II-a** — 1858 (1 Nov 1858 – 5 May 1862), bull's head + star in a
  round-cornered rectangle, Latin-letter *Porto Gazetei* (5 P) / *Porto Scrisorei*
  (40, 80 P), "PARALE" in Cyrillic; three values 5 / 40 / 80 parale
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 4).

## Numbering system

Sequential **`L.P.`** (Lista Poștală) numbers:

- Issue I = **L.P. 1–4** (1 = 27 P, 2 = 54 P, 3 = 81 P, 4 = 108 P)
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3).
- Issue II = **L.P. 5–7**, with paper/frame sub-numbers — 5 (5a, 5b), 6 (6a),
  7 (7a, 7b, 7c) — and lettered shade lines beneath
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photos 4–6).

**Valuations in 1984 Romanian Lei**, columns **`*`** (unused), **`⊙`** (used),
**`✉`** (on cover). "on-cover" and many rarities are quoted **`p.a`** =
*preț de amator* (negotiated / on-request). Prices refer to **square-cut copies with
large margins**; round- or octagonal-cut pieces are worth **25 %** of those
([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3).

### Bull's Head cotes (1984 Lei) — as read from the photos

Issue I (L.P. 1–4), unused `*` / used `⊙` (cover = p.a for all)
([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3):

| № | Denom. | unused * | used ⊙ |
|---|--------|---------:|-------:|
| 1 | 27 P negru / h. roz pal | 330 000 | 100 000 |
| 2 | 54 P albastru / h. verzuie | 75 000 | 40 000 |
| 3 | 81 P albastru / h. albăstruie | 260 000 | 280 000 |
| 4 | 108 P albastru / h. roz deschis | 200 000 | 80 000 |

Issue II (L.P. 5–7), representative base shades, unused `*` / used `⊙`
([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photos 4–6):

| № | Denom. / shade | unused * | used ⊙ |
|---|----------------|---------:|-------:|
| 5.I.a | 5 P negru, h. albăstruie, cadru complet (1858) | 330 000 | 100 000 |
| 5.I.b | 5 P h. albă (1859) | 250 000 | 80 000 |
| 5.I.c | 5 P h. gălbuie (1859) | 260 000 | 90 000 |
| 5.II | 5 P cadru spart (1862), h. albă | 2 000 | — |
| 6.II.a | 40 P albastru, h. albă/gri (1859) | 2 500 | 1 800 |
| 6.I.a | 40 P albastru, h. albăstruie | 3 000 | 2 500 |
| 7.I.a | 80 P cărămiziu, h. albă, tip II/III (1858) | 5 500 | 3 000 |
| 7.III | 80 P h. albăstruie (azure), 1861 | 250 000 | 7 500 |

> Note the same used > unused inversion on **81 P** (280 000 vs 260 000) that the
> other catalogues show — used copies are the rarer state (only 709 sold). The
> **azure-paper 80 P** (1861) is the standout mint rarity at 250 000 Lei
> ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photos 3, 6).

27 P 6 016 (sold 3 691); 54 P 10 016 (sold 4 772); 81 P 2 016 (sold 709);

## What it uniquely offers

- The most granular **shade tables** per paper (5.I; 6.I/6.II/6.III; 7.I/7.II/7.III),
  each with tête-bêche, double/blind-impression and unit-record lines
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photos 4–6).
- **Largest-known-multiple** records (e.g. issue II unit = sheet of 32 with 8
  tête-bêche unused, strip of 4 used; 40 P block of 8 / strip of 4)
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photos 4, 5).
- Circulation notes: issue-II stamps circulated only inside Moldavia, are rarer used
  in Muntenia, and 40/80 P cancelled *FRANCO 1859* carry a premium; Cyrillic-FRANCO
  cancels are collectors' pieces ("piese de amator")
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 5).
- Explicit warning: **no essays exist** — items sold as essays are forgeries
  ([[../../sources/spineanu-catalog-marci-1984/text|Spineanu]] photo 3).
