# Kiriac Dragomir — *Catalogul mărcilor poștale românești* (1974)

The dominant Romanian reference catalogue for Romanian stamps (623 pp, Bucharest,
1974), including microscopic forgery-distinguishing marks. The **"Kiriac figure"**
numbers for classic postmarks are the *de facto* public reference cited across auction
catalogues (see [[../postmarks/census]]) — which is why this source is central to the
[[../handstamps/PM52|PM52]]/[[../handstamps/M1|M1]] → Kiriac concordance.
