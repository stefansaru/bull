# Chiricheș & Pascariu — *Catalogul mărcilor poștale românești 1858–2000*, vol. I

A Romanian catalogue by **Coriolan Chiricheș** and **Samuel Pascariu** (publisher
**AXA**), vol. I covering **1858–2000**, numbered as one continuous **postal list
`L.P. 1 – L.P. 1538`** ([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]]
photo 2). Its distinguishing feature for the Bull's Head is the **concordance to the
international catalogues** — **Yvert (Yv), Michel (Mi) and Scott (S)** — printed
against the Romanian numbers, plus the **1858 gold-franc parity** of each
denomination. It also lists **service, postage-due, charity, aviation-fund and
fiscal-postal** issues (photo 2).

> **Year unknown.** No legible imprint date on the photographed pages; the "1858–2000"
> span places publication in/after 2000. Confirm against the copyright page.

Terminology: uses **"Cap de bour (Cap de zimbru)"** — both names together
([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photo 3).

## What it covers (Bull's Head)

- **Emisiunea I** — 1858, **22 July – 31 October**, Moldova, *Porto Scrisori* in
  Cyrillic, printed at Iași piece-by-piece, coloured paper (horizontally laid for
  27/54/108, plain for 81), circulated only in Moldavia
  ([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photo 3).
- **Emisiunea a II-a** — 1858 Nov 1 – 1862 May, *Porto Gazetei* (5 P) /
  *Porto Scrisorei* (40, 80 P), value in Cyrillic PAR(ale)
  ([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photos 3, 5).

## Numbering system (L.P. + international concordance)

The Bull's Heads are **L.P. 1–7**, with lettered sub-variants
([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photos 3, 5):

| Issue | L.P. № | Yvert | Michel | Scott |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|-------|
| I (27/54/81/108 P) | **1–4** | Yv 1–4 | Mi 1–4 | S 1–4 |
| II (5/40/80 P) | **5–7** | Yv 5–7 | Mi 5x–7x | S 5–7 |

Sub-numbering: **1 = 27 P, 2 = 54 P, 3 = 81 P, 4 = 108 P**; then **5 / 5a / 5b / 5c**
(5 P: blue-paper full frame / white full frame / white broken frame [unissued] /
tête-bêche), **6 / 6a–6e** (40 P shades + tête-bêche), **7 / 7a–7d** (80 P shades)
([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photo 5).

Uniquely, it prints the **1858 parity** of each value: **27 P = 25 centime,
54 P = 50 centime, 81 P = 75 centime, 108 P = 1 gold French franc**
([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photo 3).

## What it uniquely offers

- The only one of the three to print a **Yvert / Michel / Scott concordance** for the
  Bull's Heads, and the **1858 gold-franc parity** of each denomination
  ([[../../sources/chirices-pascariu-catalog/text|Chiricheș-Pascariu]] photo 3).
- A single continuous **L.P. 1–1538** numbering across 1858–2000, extending well
  beyond the classics into service/postage-due/fiscal-postal material (photo 2).
