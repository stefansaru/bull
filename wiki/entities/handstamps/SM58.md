# 🐂 SM58 — Seal of the Directorate of Posts and Diligences (1858)

The **1858 seal of the Moldavian postal directorate** — struck in the very year of
the *Cap de bour* stamps, and carrying the same **bull's head** in full heraldic
achievement. In 1858 the directorate also issued the first seals to the individual
expedition bureaus (`SM58 CAHUL`, `SM58 BOLGRAD`, `SM58 ISMAIL`)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 20–21).

**Device.** An **oval** seal, **30 × 33 mm**. In the field a **rectangular shield
bearing the *cap de bour*** (bull's head), the shield **timbred with a closed
crown**; **two dolphins affronté** surround the shield; **below, a postal horn**
with a wide-open circle enclosing the year **1858**. Round the border, the legend:
**"DIREKȚIA POSTELOR ȘI A DELIJANSELOR"**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 28, photo
20). A **negative** variant is catalogued as **`SM58a`** (Fig. 29), and the bureau
seals — e.g. `SM58 CAHUL` (Fig. 30, incised oval, legend "PRINCIPAT: MOLDOVEI") and
`SM58 BOLGRAD` (Fig. 31, oval) — repeat the arms with the office name
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 20–21). A
`SM58 IZMAIL` appears on a postal receipt (*adeverință*) of 2 November 1860 (Fig.
38, photo 24).

The **"și a Delijanselor"** (and of the Diligences) in the title fixes the moment:
the directorate had just absorbed the diligence enterprise — the same 1857 buy-back
that produced the [[M1]] and [[C1]] Vienna handstamps — as the state took the post
in hand on the eve of the stamp.
