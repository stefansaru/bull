# 🐂 SM55 — Seal of the Central Expedition of Posts (1855)

The 1855 renewal of the Moldavian expedition seals, catalogued by Dragomir under
`SM55` in **two forms**: the **central seal** of the *Expediția Centrală a Poștelor*
at Iași (Fig. 8) and the matching **district-town expedition seals** (Fig. 22)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 18–19).

**Central seal (Fig. 8).** An **oval**, **30 × 35 mm**, with a **double frame** (the
first line plain, the outer one **pearled**). In the field the **current official
arms** (*cap de bour* shield with dolphins) and, below the dolphins, the **postal
horn**. The legend in **Cyrillic cursive**: **"Principatul Moldaviei: 1855 · Expediția
Centrală a Poștelor"** (R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 8, photo 19).

**District-town seals (Fig. 22).** Same form, size and composition as the central
seal; the legend in **Cyrillic majuscules**, e.g. **"EKSPEDIȚIA POȘTII TÎRGU
FOKȘANII 1855"**. These town seals equipped the same 13 expedition points as the
1852 series
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 22, photo 19):
**Bacău** (R), **Bârlad** (R), **Botoșani** (R), **Dorohoi** (R), **Fălticeni** (1,5R),
**Focșani** (R), **Galați** (R), **Huși** (1,5R), **Mihăileni** (5R), **Piatra**
(1,5R), **Roman** (R), **Tecuci** (1,5R), **Vaslui** (1,5R).
