# 🦅 SV30 — Seal of the State Expedition, Wallachia

Dragomir's §1.2.1 (*Expediția Statului*) — the seal used on the Wallachian
**state-expedition** postal forms, struck by the Casa Poștelor (the Directorate) or
by a post station's captain acting in the contractor's name
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 33–34). To
travel by post-horse one needed a **podorojnă** (passport-permit); the form carried
either a printed seal-handstamp or a negative soot-stamp / wax seal, according to case.

**Device.** A **round** seal, **37 mm**, legend **"EXPEDIȚIA PRINCIPATULUI VALAHII"**,
without indication of the year (rarity **5R**; a negative variant **10R**)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 58, photos
33–34).
