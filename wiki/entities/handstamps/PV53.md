# 🦅 PV53 — Wallachian bilinear dated pre-philatelic handstamps (1853–1862)

The author's code **`PV53`** (Dragomir §1.2.2, *"Ștampile 'Biliniare datate'"*)
denotes the **two-line, frameless day-datestamps of Wallachia** (Țara Românească /
"muntene") used through the pre-stamp period and into the early classic era
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 129).

**Device.** A **double-line handstamp, without a frame**: the **first line** carries
the locality (post-office name) in **Cyrillic majuscules**; **below** it the **date**
— the month by its first three letters (smaller) and the day, in Arabic figures.
Two devices appear later and in **Latin letters** (**BRAILA**, 1861; **PLOESCI**,
1862) but are still catalogued under PV53
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 129).

**Catalogued extent.** **26 handstamps**, whose rarity (hence philatelic value) is
estimated from the number of surviving examples in known collections. The **overall
date span is 6 March 1853** (earliest, col. 4 = București mare) **to 21 July 1862**
(latest, col. 11 = Foksani); per-device early/late dates and full dimensions
(L / H / W / S / h) are tabulated in **Tabel 2 (APV53)**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 129–130).

*(No clean single-device crop of a PV53 bilinear datestamp is reproduced here; the
26 impressions are on Planșa 16 and the seven covering letters on Planșa 17,
Fig. 260–266 — [[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]]
photos 129, 131–132.)*

## Plate 17 — the seven illustrating letters

Dragomir's **Planșa 17** presents *"un ansamblu din 7 scrisori prefilatelice
obliterate"* (an assembly of 7 pre-philatelic letters) cancelled with PV53 marks
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 129):

1. ZIMNICEA OKT 10 (Fig. 260)
2. KAMPULUNG NOE 20 (Fig. 261)
3. RUSI DE VEDE 4 FEB + BUKURESTI FEB 7 (Fig. 262)
4. TARGUJIUL FEB 22 (Fig. 263)
5. ZIMNICEA 25 SEP, tip I (Fig. 264)
6. TURNU AVG 3, tip I (Fig. 265)
6. TURNU SEP 10, tip II (Fig. 266) — *"6" is duplicated in the source; this is the
   seventh letter (see notes)*

## ⚠ Notes on the source (verify)

- **Plate number.** The datestamps are assigned to **Planșa 16**; the seven letters
  to **Planșa 17** ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]]
  photo 129). Confirm which plate reproduces the 26 impressions themselves.
- **Spelling drift list ↔ table.** The running list and Tabel 2 spell four towns
  differently: BUKURESTI/BUCURESTI, KIMPULUNG/KAMPULUNG, RIMNIKU VILCEA/RĂMNIKU
  VALCEA, TIRGOVISTE·TIRGUJIUL/TARGOVISTE·TARGUJIU, PLOESCI/PLOESTI (Latin) (photos
  129–130).
- **Numbering.** The Plate-17 list runs 1,2,3,4,5,6,**6** — a duplicated "6" for
  seven stated letters (photo 129).
