# 🦅 CpV — personal seals of the Wallachian post-captains

Dragomir's **§1.2.4 *Căpitanii de poștă*** (`CpV` = *Căpitani, Valahia*). Each post
station was run by a *căpitan de poștă*, who besides the station's own seal
([[SV-stations]]) used a **personal seal** — often heraldic, bearing his monogram or
initials and family arms rather than the eagle. These CpV seals appear on letters,
*miziluri* and *ștafete* with the same function as the "official" station devices; the
**feather-secured** versions (*sigilii cu pene*) are rare and highly prized
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] §1.2.4, photos
38–40). Dragomir names as examples of CpV: **Negoiești, Rusi de Vede, Circinov**.

## Context

The captain (*om vrednic și știutor de carte* — "capable and literate") answered for
his relay's horses, carts and *surugii*. His personal seal, closing a *ștafetă* or
*mizil* in red wax — sometimes over a spray of feathers to signal urgency — is both a
postal-history document and, in the feather form, a spectacular exhibition piece
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 39–40; and
[[../concepts/menzil-relay-system]]).
