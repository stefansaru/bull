# 🐂 CPM — Seals of the Moldavian post-captains (*căpitanii de poștă*)

The personal seals of the **post-captains (*căpitanii de poștă*)** who ran individual
relay stations — small, often improvised devices with the captain's monogram or a
local emblem, spanning roughly **1845–1858**. Dragomir treats them in **§1.1.5** and
gathers six on **Plate 5 (Fig. 51–56): Adjud, Movila, Solești, Rădeni, Peneu,
Turcești** ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Planșa
5, photos 26–30).

**Character.** These are not the uniform, arms-bearing directorate seals but the
captains' own devices: many carry only **initials in Cyrillic majuscules** in a plain
field; a few show heraldic charges — a **shield with the *cap de bour*** (Podu
Iloaiei), or shields with a galloping courier (Rădeni, Turcești). Sizes are small,
from ~8 mm rings to ~38 mm ovals.
