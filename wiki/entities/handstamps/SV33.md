# 🦅 SV33 — Seal of the House of Posts, Wallachia (1833)

The 1833 seal of the Wallachian **Casa Poștelor** (House of Posts), applied *în fum*
(soot) — a fully pictorial device of the Wallachian post
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 62, photo
34).

**Device.** An **oval** seal, **41 × 38 mm**. In the field, the **post cart carrying
a traveller, moving to the right, drawn by four horses with a mounted postillion**;
above, the **cross-bearing eagle (*acvila cruciată*) in flight, head to the right**;
the Cyrillic legend runs round the margin with the year **1833**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 34). It
belongs to the three-year lease series (*trienalele 1830 și 1833*) before the
five-year exploitations of 1836 onward
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 32).
