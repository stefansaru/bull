# 🐂 SM27 — Seal of the Menzil House of Moldavia (1827)

The second attested seal of the **Casa Menzilului Moldovei**, corresponding to the
1827 relay lease. Across the turbulent 1820s the Menzil House used two attested dies
matching successive three-year terms; `SM27` is the second
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 12).

**Device.** A **double-circle** seal, **33 and 24 mm**, with **14 exterior
festoons**; in the centre a postillion (*surugiu*) on the road **between two
trees**, sounding a horn, the whip in the left hand. The exergue carries the
**Cyrillic majuscule** legend and millesime: **"PECETE KĂSII MENZILULUI MOLDAVIEI
1827"**, the stop an **eight-ray star**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 12).
