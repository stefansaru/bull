# ☎ ST — telegraph-office seals (coded TL)

Dragomir's **§2.4 *Ștampile Telegrafice* («ST»)** — the seals of the **telegraph
service**. The first lines were built in **1854/55**; the first telegraph-station chief was
the Belgian sergeant **Cezar Librecht**, at Galați in 1854, and by the start of **1859
there were only 16 telegraph stations open for correspondence** (11 in Moldavia, 5 in
Muntenia), staffed by borrowed Austrian officials who were replaced that year by natives
under a Cuza decree ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]]
photo 96). Eleven seals are catalogued (**Planșa 13, Fig. 185–191**), all coded **`TL`**.
Context at **[[../concepts/auxiliary-sigillary-stamps]]**.

> **Code caution.** The section header names the class **`ST`**, but **every device is
> coded `TL`** (TL55…TL71) and Planșa 13's caption reads *"Ștampile telegrafice («TL»)"* —
> the letter `ST` is never actually used in the catalogue (see inconsistencies).

## Notes & inconsistencies

> ⚠ **`ST` header vs `TL` codes.** §2.4 is headed *"Ștampile Telegrafice («ST»)"* (photo
> 96), but all eleven devices are coded **TL** and the Planșa 13 caption reads *"«TL»"*
> (photo 102). The class letter `ST` is not used anywhere in the catalogue.

- **`TL59` covers three different devices** — Inspectoratul General (Fig. 185), Rîmnicul
  Sărat (Fig. 191) and Jassy (Fig. 189); likewise **`TL55`** covers two (Direcția Moldovei,
  Galați) and **`TL58`** two (Focșani, Tecuci). Consistent with Dragomir's year-based
  coding, but the codes disambiguate only by office/figure.
- **Spelling.** Entry 3 is printed **"ISPECTORATUL GENERAL"** in the body (photo 98) but
  **"INSPECTORATUL"** in the Planșa 13 caption and in the seal's own legend (photo 102) —
  a missing *N* in the body text.
- **Figure gap at 184.** §2.3 (Planșa 12) ends at Fig. 183 and §2.4 (Planșa 13) opens at
  Fig. 185; **Fig. 184 is not located in these sections** ⚠ (verify — it may be a
  document/map elsewhere).
