# 🦅 Sigilii cu pene — the feather-secured express seals

Dragomir's **§1.2.5 *Sigilii cu pene (ștafete și miziluri)***. A **feather seal** is not
a distinct device but a **mode of application**: the wax closing an urgent dispatch was
pressed over a **spray of feathers or a quill** (*pene* / *pană de porumbel*, a pigeon
feather) fanned out under the seal (*în evantai*). The protruding feathers were a visual
"rush" signal for the mounted couriers — the ***ștafetari*** — who carried express
messages along the relay ([[../concepts/menzil-relay-system]]).

The device pressed into the wax could be a station seal ([[SV-stations]]) or a captain's
personal seal ([[CpV]]); what makes the piece a *sigiliu cu pene* is the feathers. Such
covers are **rare, valuable postal-history documents and spectacular exhibition pieces**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] §1.2.5, photos 40–44).

## The class

The first Wallachian *sigilii cu pene* are known from the **occupation of the Romanian
Principalities** by the belligerent **Russian, Turkish and Austrian** armies engaged in
the **Crimean War (1853–1856)**. They occur on *miziluri* and *ștafete* carrying urgent
dispatches or messages, transported by couriers on horseback known as *ștafetari*
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 40). Dragomir
refers the reader to his own article *Ștafete, miziluri și sigilii cu pene* in
*Filatelia* no. 1/1988 for detail.

## Reference pieces (exemplificări)

1. **Ștafeta (Fig. 99), 31 May 1854** — an early date. The cover bears **two feather
   seals** fixed under red wax by the local captain's [[CpV]] **Știrbei** seal (Fig. 98).
   The urgency is echoed in the endorsement: *"Ștafetă, ștafetă foarte grabnică. Pornită
   luni la 31 mai, la ceasurile patru dimineața"* ("express, very urgent express;
   dispatched Monday 31 May, at four in the morning"), addressed to Bucharest (col. 9)
   ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 40).

2. **Mizil (Fig. 100), 31 November 1854** — a printed form carrying **express no. 686**,
   sent by the **K.K. Agenție (Agency) in Bucharest to the Carantina Timiș** (Timiș
   quarantine). The feathers are fixed under wax by the [[SV51]] **Direcția Poștelor
   Drumului Ploiești** seal; the verso records the relay of *ștafetari* (ASB.PTR. dosar
   nr. 15/1854, fila 36)
   ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 40).

3. **Mizil (Fig. 101), 4 March 1855** — a manuscript form carrying the feather seal of
   the **Greci** post station ([[SV-stations]], SV54 Greci). It conveys an **Austrian
   military express** addressed to the command in Bucharest (ASB.PTR. dosar nr. 14/1854,
   fila 197) ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 40).

4. **Ștafeta (Fig. 103), 2 June 1856** — a wrapper (*învălitoare*) endorsed *"Ștafetă,
   ștafetă, grabnică, grabnică, foarte grabnică"*. Sender: the captain of the Știrbei
   post, addressed *"Cinstitei Direcții a Drumului Știrbeiu"*. A **pigeon feather** is
   fixed under wax, fanned out, by the [[SV51]] **Călărași** station seal (Fig. 87)
   (col. 9) ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 40).
