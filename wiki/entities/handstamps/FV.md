# 🦅 FV — sigillary handstamps of the Wallachian leased road-post

Dragomir's **`FV`** family (§1.3.1) is the set of **sigillary handstamps struck on the
printed transport forms** (*formulare de transport*) of the **leased Wallachian
road-post**, 1833–1861. The seals are **round** (oval by exception), **single- or
double-circle**, of the **sigillary type, sometimes negative**; the field shows the
**post cart drawn by four horses** with a mounted postillion, and above it the
**crowned, cross-bearing eagle** in flight (sword and mace in its talons), the **year**
or a postal horn below the road-line
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 44). The forms
are large sheets framed in ornamental borders **Md. I – Md. XIII**. See the concept
page **[[../concepts/franco-porto-handstamps]]** for the family and the "Franco" naming
caution. These `FV` road-administration seals are **distinct from the town post-station
seals** ("ПОШТА [town] АНУЛ [year]") catalogued separately at
[[SV-stations]].
