# ⊛ M3 — Moldavia FRANCO obliterators (1859)

**`M3`** is Dragomir's class of **FRANCO obliterating handstamps of 1859** — the
"franco" (prepaid) marks that were also used to cancel stamps
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]], ABREVIERI photo
8; §M3 photos 156–158). Dragomir notes that **"a few M3. FRANCO handstamps are rare
and receive special point-values"** and gives worked examples such as **M3. JASSY
tip. II** and mixed usages with an **M1t. JASSY** on the same cover
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 157–158).
Because the FRANCO mark denoted prepayment, its appearance *obliterating* a stamp is
a notable combination and a driver of the census's rarer strikes.
