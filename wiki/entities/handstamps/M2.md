# ◉ M2 — Moldavia large round obliterators (1859)

**`M2`** is the family of **large "MOLDOVA" double-circle handstamps of 1859** —
Dragomir's *§2.2 Ștampilele "MOLDOVA" mari din 1859* — double-circle devices of
about **25 × 13 mm**, the locality and "MOLDOVA" set in the exergue with a
separating dash
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]], "M2. MOLDOVA,
mari (Pl. 19)", photo 145). They came into use from **27 April 1859** and ran into
the mid-1860s; Dragomir tabulates early/late dates per bureau, closing with the note
that the **M2. KILIA** device is *"neutilizat"* (recorded unused) (photos 146–147).

They are the larger successors to the small [[M1]] cancels and obliterate the
second *Cap de bour* issue as well as later Moldavian mail.
