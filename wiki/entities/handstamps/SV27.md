# 🦅 SV27 — Seal of the Menzil Houses of Wallachia (1827)

The Wallachian counterpart of the Moldavian [[SM22]]/[[SM27]] — the seal of the
**Casa Menzilurilor Valahiei** (House of the Relay-posts of Wallachia), the
central administration of the Wallachian post
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] §1.2.2, photo
34). Where the Moldavian seals carry the bull's head, the Wallachian device carries
the **eagle** — the emblem of Țara Românească.

**Device.** An **oval** seal, **41 × 38 mm**. In the field, the **post cart in
motion to the left, drawn by two horses with a postillion (*surugiu*) whipping them
on at the gallop**; above and in front, the **cross-bearing eagle (*acvila
cruciată*) in flight**. The legend is in **Cyrillic majuscules**: **"PECETE KĂSII
MENZILURILOR WALACHII 1827"**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 34, sec.
"Administrația centrală").

## Context — the seven post roads

From Bucharest ran **seven post roads** toward the frontiers, their terminal points
being Danube ports (**quarantines** from 1830) or Carpathian customs — Focșani (with
a Brăila branch), Călărași (Știrbei from 1853), Giurgiu, Craiova (five branches),
Câineni, Tîrgoviște–Câmpulung and Ploiești (extended to Predeal in 1854). Relay
stations sat about every 16 km under a *căpitan de poștă*; the 1811 Scufa contract
provided thousands of horses, *surugii*, wheelwrights and drivers
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 30–31; and
[[../concepts/menzil-relay-system]]).
