# 🐂 SM22 — Seal of the Menzil House of Moldavia (1822)

The **earliest known Moldavian postal marking** and the point at which Dragomir's
whole catalogue — and this wiki's pre-stamp record — begins. `SM22` is the seal of
the **Casa Menzilului Moldovei** (the relay-post house; see
[[../concepts/menzil-relay-system]]), used under the postal lease held by the Greek
contractor **C. Scufa** (*trienalul antreprizei Scufa, 1822/25*).

**Device.** Within a **double frame**, the field shows a mounted courier
(*surugiu / timiraș*) holding the reins in the right hand and the whip in the left;
the legend runs in **Cyrillic majuscules** with the date in the millesime:
**"PECETEA KĂSII MENZILULUI MOLDAVIEI 1822"**, the stop marked by an ornament
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 11).

**Survival.** Dragomir records it as **"exemplar unic"** — a single known example —
on a **postal order (dispoziția nr. 24) of 7 September 1825** addressed to the
Dorohoi post, arranging a four-horse cart and postillion for "Prințul Iorgu,
princely financial inspector," to travel to Iași with the transport toll (*ugerit*)
charged to the village account; in the collection of **Fritz Heimbüchler**, Munich
(Dragomir Fig. 1) ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]]
photos 11, 13).
