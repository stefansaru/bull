# 🐂 SPM59 — Post-station seals of the "Drumul Mare", Moldavia (1859)

The uniform series of **relay-station seals (Stațiile de poștă, SPM)** cut in **1859**
for the stations along the **"Drumul Mare"** (the Great Road), under the postal lease
of **Mavrogheni & Sufu**. Dragomir opens **§1.1.4 (Stațiile de poștă)** with the type
example, **Poșta Solești** (Fig. 39)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 39, photo 25).

**Device (Poșta Solești, Fig. 39).** A **round** seal, **35 mm**. The border is a
**string of pearls (140)** set between two thin lines, the outer forming the rim. In
the field, a **horse at the gallop, to the left**; under the road-line the initials
**"M&S"** in Antiqua majuscules (for the lessees **Mavrogheni și Sufu**) and below
them the year **1859**. In the exergue, the legend in **Cyrillic majuscules**:
**"PRINCIPATUL MOLDAVIEI"** between two six-lobed floral ornaments, and below
**"POȘTA SOLEȘTI"**. Found struck **in wax (4R)** or **in soot/ink (8R)**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 39, photo 25).

**Variant SPM59a — Movila (Fig. 41).** A **round** seal, **35 mm**, of form and
composition like the station type, **engraved in incision for striking in ink (4R)**;
rarely also **in wax (8R)**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 41, photo 26).

**The 17 stations.** Catalogued under `SPM59`
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 25):
1. Bârlad · 2. Berheci · 3. Bunești · 4. Crasna · 5. Docolina · 6. Fundeni ·
7. Movila · 8. Peneu · 9. Pereschiv · 10. Poenile · 11. Poiana Crimului ·
12. Sardari · 13. Serbești · 14. Solești · 15. Strîmtura · 16. Turcești (Fig. 40) ·
17. Galați — all seals of stations along the *Drumul Mare*.
