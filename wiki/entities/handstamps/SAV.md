# 🛃 SAV — customs-house seals (1830–1910)

Dragomir's **§2.5 *Ștampile Vamale* («SAV»)** — the official seals of the **customs houses**
(*vămi*, formerly *schele*), set on the frontier and only exceptionally in the interior
(București, Iași), struck **in wax or as negative smoke/ink impressions on documents and
letters** ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 103,
106). Dragomir catalogues **72 offices** in three geographic groups and figures eight
device examples (**Planșa 14, Fig. 193–200**). Context at
**[[../concepts/auxiliary-sigillary-stamps]]**.

> **Code caution.** The class is headed **`SAV`**, but the device descriptions code them
> **`SV`** (SV.GALATI, SV.DORNA, SV.VIRCIOROVA, SV.SOVEJA) while the cross-references and
> the Planșa 14 caption use **`SAV`** (see inconsistencies). `SV` also collides with
> **SV = Sigilare-Valahia** (postal seals) elsewhere in the book.

## Notes & inconsistencies

> ⚠ **`SAV` header vs `SV` codes.** The devices are coded **SV** in the text but **SAV** in
> the cross-references and the Planșa 14 caption; e.g. **Dorna** is *SV.DORNA* in the text
> (photo 106) yet *SAV.DORNA* on the plate (photo 107).

> ⚠ **Plate 14 caption vs text.** The descriptive text assigns Fig. 193 Dorna, **194
> Cubei**, 195 Vîlcov, **196 Bicaz**, **197 Soveja**, **198 Cornu Luncii**, **199 Tatar
> Bunar**, 200 Kilia. The Planșa 14 caption pairs these figure numbers with a **different
> order of offices** (Bicaz / Cubei / Cornu Luncii / Tatar Bunar / Soveja permuted) —
> e.g. the caption reads Fig. 197 as Tatar Bunar, the text as Soveja. Verify against a
> clean copy (photo 107).

- **Duplicate office entries.** **Bicaz (Neamț)** is listed twice — **#46 and #49**;
  **Dărăbani (Dorohoi)** twice — **#58 and #62** (photos 103–104). Likely duplications (or
  one is a differently-named locality misprinted).
- **Numbering error on the eastern line.** The "Linia convențională" sublist runs
  **63(?), 69, 70, 71, 72** — the first should be **68**; as printed, **63 duplicates
  Ștefănești** (already #63) and **68 is missing** (photo 103).
- **Note's SAV numbers vs list positions.** In the Planșa 14 note, **SAV 20 Kilia,
  SAV 21 Vîlcov, SAV 46 Bicaz** match the list positions (#20, #21, #46), but **SAV 49
  Cornu Luncii, SAV 64 Cubei, SAV 65 Tatar Bunar** do **not** — those offices are #53, #71
  and #72 in the list (photos 103, 107).
- The class letter **`SAV`** is not in the front *ABREVIERI* list; it appears only in the
  §2.5 header.
