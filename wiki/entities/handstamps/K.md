# 🩺 K — quarantine seals of the Danube cordon (1830–1859)

The **sanitary seals of the Danube quarantine cordon** — struck on mail disinfected at
the *carantine* — catalogued by Dragomir under **`K`** (the initial of **"Karantină"**),
followed by the last two digits of the seal's first year of use and the station name
(e.g. **`K30 CERNEȚI`** = the first, 1830, seal of the Cerneți quarantine)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 71). Dragomir
records **70 known Romanian quarantine stamps, 6 of them unencountered**; Racoviceanu
(1963) had described only six Wallachian examples
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 71). The full
context is at **[[../concepts/quarantine-posts]]**.

## The `K30` type and its variants (1830)

On **1 January 1830** all twelve Wallachian Danube quarantines were furnished with
matrices of the **`K30` type BRĂILA**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 127, photo 71):

- **Device.** A **round** stamp, **24 mm**. In the emblem, the **cross-bearing,
  crowned eagle in flight (wings open) on a green terrace**, head reversed; two plants
  flanking the terrace; above the eagle the **sun (as an eight-lobed rosette)**; **no
  year**. Round the circle, the legend in capitals: **"KARANTINA BRĂILA"**.
- **Variants:** **K30a** (variants of K30), **K30b** ("*Clasa a doua*" — 2nd-class
  quarantines), **K30c** ("*Oboare de schimb*" — exchange stations), **K30d** ("*Direcția
  carantinei*" — the Directorate)
  ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 71).

The twelve stations equipped in 1830 include **CERNEȚI, IZVOARELE, CALAFAT, OLTENIȚA,
IZLAZ, TURNU, ZIMNICEA, CĂLĂRAȘI, GIURGIU, BECHET** … (each with a rarity/points
notation such as "10R", "15R", "5R", "FF" in the source "⚠ — parenthetical values
OCR-only")
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 71).

Later station seals run through the code range **`K31`–`K59`** (1831–1859). Attested
examples in the text: **`K34`** and, from 1838, **`K38`** at **Gura Ialomiței**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 64); the
**`K38` Severin** seal appears on the unique certificate of disinfection "*prin afumare
la carantina Severin, 13 oct. 1838*" (Fig. 125, col. 9)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 64).

## The stations — "TABELUL K"

Dragomir's **TABELUL K** lists the quarantines **in the order of the Danube's course**
against the `K30`–`K59` columns
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 70):

| # | Station | # | Station | # | Station |
|---|---------|---|---------|---|---------|
| 1 | Cerneți | 8 | Turnu (Măgurele) | 15 | Gura Ialomiței |
| 2 | Turnu Severin | 9 | Zimnicea | 16 | Brăila |
| 3 | Izvoarele | 10 | Giurgiu | 17 | Galați I |
| 4 | Gruia | 11 | Oltenița | 18 | Galați II |
| 5 | Calafat | 12 | Călărași | 19 | Reni |
| 6 | Bechet | 13 | Stîrbei | 20 | Ismail |
| 7 | Izlaz | 14 | Piua Pietrii | 21 | Chilia |

Stations 1–16 are the Wallachian Danube cordon (Vîrciorova → the Ialomița mouth);
**17–21 (Galați I, Galați II, Reni, Ismail, Chilia)** are the Moldavian / lower-Danube
ports under the **Direcția Carantinei** at Galați
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 66–70). The
seat-changes of 1834–1852 (Piua Petrii → Gura Ialomiței; Călărași renamed Stîrbei;
Cerneți → Turnu Severin) explain the paired entries
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 59–61).

*(The full station-by-code matrix of which `K`-code was used at each quarantine is
given by the dot-grid of TABELUL K in the source; it is not transcribed cell-by-cell
here.)*
