# 🐂 SM46 — Seal of the Directorate of Posts of Moldavia (1846)

The seal of the newly-created **Direcția Poștelor Moldovei** (Directorate of Posts),
established at Iași when in **January 1846** the Moldavian posts were leased out
"by roads" (*pe drumuri*), superseding the old *Casa Menzilului* arrangement. It
opens Dragomir's section **§1.1.2 (Direcția Poștelor și Birourile districtuale,
1846–1858)** and is the **first Moldavian postal device to carry the full national
arms** — the *cap de bour* set in a heraldic achievement with the postal horn
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 7, photos
14–15).

**Device.** An **oval** seal, **27 × 40 mm**, with a **double frame** (3 mm apart).
In the field a **rectangular shield bearing the *cap de bour*** (bull's head) with a
**star between the horns**; the shield **supported by two dolphins affronté**, and
below them the **postal horn**. Round the exergue, the legend in **Cyrillic
majuscules** with the millesime, reading approximately **"PECETE DIREKȚIA POȘTELOR
PRINCIPATUL MOLDOVII 1846"** ⚠ (OCR — verify exact spelling)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 7, photos
14–15). Rarity (R).

**Context.** Dragomir stresses that the postal horn — the universal postal symbol —
"appears for the first time in 1846" on the directorate's seal, and that the new
emblem combines it with the arms of the country (the *cap de bour*, an official
attribute of the state administration since the start of the century). The
directorate ran under a director and secretary, with district bureaus under the
county *sameși* (treasurers)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 15).
