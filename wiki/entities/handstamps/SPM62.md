# 🐂 SPM62 — Post-station seal of Focșani, United Principalities (1862)

The **1862 station seal of Focșani**, notable because it carries the **united arms**
in a striking form: the **eagle bearing the *cap de bour* on its breast**. Dragomir
catalogues it as `SPM62` with wax and round variants and a related Capu Dracului seal
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 42, photo 26).

**Device (SPM62, Fig. 42).** An **oval** seal, **34 × 30 mm**. In the field the
**crowned cross-bearing eagle (*acvila cruciată și încoronată*) with the *cap de bour*
on its breast**; at the eagle's feet, the **postal horn**. The legend in **Roman
capitals**: **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE"** (above) and, on two curved lines below,
**"STAȚIA POȘTALE / DIN FOKSANI"**, the place-name between **two six-ray stars**; the
year **1862** in two digits flanking the horn (3R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 42, photo 26).

**Variants.**
- **SPM62a** — oval **34 × 30 mm**, similar composition, legend **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE
  / STAȚIEA POȘTALE / DIN FOKSANI"**; struck in **wax** (3R).
- **SPM62b** — **round 30 mm**; the crowned cross-eagle **holds the *cap de bour* with
  its muzzle resting on the horn**; legend **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE / STAȚIA POȘTALE /
  DIN FOKSAN"** (3R)
  ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 26).

**Related — SPM62c, Poșta Capu Dracului (Fig. 48).** A **hexagonal** seal, **35 × 25
mm** (from a rectangle with cut corners). In the upper half, divided by a floriated
bar, **three ligatured letters in Cyrillic majuscules "P.J.R."**; in the lower field,
the legend on two lines **"POȘTA / KAPU DRACULUI"** (5R). A similar seal exists for
**"POȘTA · ORAȘA"** with the initials **"C.A.C."** (Fig. 49)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 26).
