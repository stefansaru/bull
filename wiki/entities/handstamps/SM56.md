# 🐂 SM56 — Seal of the Administration of Posts, "Jassy" (1856)

The seal of the **Administrația Poștelor** at Iași — unusual in the Moldavian series
for **carrying no heraldic emblem at all**, only lettering. Dragomir catalogues it as
`SM56` with a **negative** variant `SM56a`
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 26–27,
photos 19–20).

**Device (SM56, Fig. 26).** A **round** seal, **35 mm**, with a **tight double
circle**. **No emblem**; round the border, in **Latin majuscules**, the inscription
**"ADMINISTRAȚIA POSTELOR"**, and **in the centre, in neo-Gothic letters, "Jassy"**
(the German/Latin form of *Iași*). Below, a **rich, long clasp** (*agrafă*) (R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 26, photo 19).

**Variant SM56a (Fig. 27).** A **negative** strike, **round 35 mm**, of similar form,
size and composition; it differs in the drawing of the **clasp**, here traced in
**thin, widely-open flourishes** and, overall, **shorter** (R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 27, photo 20).
