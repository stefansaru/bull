# 📮 SAD — district sigillary seals of Moldavia (1824–1831)

Dragomir's **§2.2 *Ștampile Sigilare Districtuale* («SAD»)** — the **first administrative
handstamps found on letters**, the negative (sigillary) seals of the Moldavian *ținut*
(county) administrations, from the period **1824–1831**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 92). Each shows
the district's own heraldic emblem and names the *ținut* in its legend. Six are catalogued,
gathered in **Planșa 11 (Fig. 173–178)**. Context at
**[[../concepts/auxiliary-sigillary-stamps]]**.

given.

## Notes & inconsistencies

- The class letter **`SAD`** is **not in the book's front *ABREVIERI* list** — it is
  introduced only in the §2.2 header
  ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 92).
- Entry 6 (**Botoșani 1831**) is described on photo 95 (after the plate) yet is figured on
  **Planșa 11** (Fig. 178) with the other five; the plate caption confirms all six
  (photo 94).
