# 📮 SAS — village sigillary seals

Dragomir's **§2.3 *Ștampile Sigilare Sătești* («SAS»)** — negative seals of **village
authorities** (schools, tax collectors: *școli, percepții*), applied to the letters those
authorities dispatched; in the philatelic period they are found **completed by a postal
dispatch/arrival cancel**
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 95). Six are
figured on **Planșa 12 (Fig. 179–183)** and three more are listed. Several carry the
**county emblem** in the field. Context at **[[../concepts/auxiliary-sigillary-stamps]]**.

## Notes & inconsistencies

> ⚠ **Entry 1 legend appears conflated.** The printed description of **Satul Haimanale
> (Fig. 179)** carries the exergue *"TANUTUL No.80 KAHUL 1859 … SATUL TOPORU"* — which
> belongs to entry 2 (**Satul Toporu**, Ținutul Cahul) — mixed with *"Buzău Pl. Câmpului /
> Satul Haimanalie"*. The two entries' legends read as if interleaved; verify against a
> clean copy ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 95).

> ⚠ **Plate 12 caption vs text.** The body text assigns **Fig. 181 = Satul Păucești**,
> **Fig. 182 = Satul Țintăreni**, **Fig. 183 = Satul Soveja**. The Planșa 12 caption appears
> to label **Fig. 181 as Satul Soveja**, duplicating Soveja (181 and 183) and dropping
> Păucești — a plate-vs-text mismatch; verify
> ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 95).

- **Satul Podoleni** (entry 4) is fully described but **carries no Fig. number** and is not
  on Planșa 12 (figs 179–183 only) — a device referenced but not illustrated.
- The class letter **`SAS`** is not in the front *ABREVIERI* list; it appears only in the
  §2.3 header.
