# ◍ M1 — Moldavia small round obliterators (1857)

**`M1`** is the family of small round **"MOLDOVA" town handstamps** — *"the first
obliterating handstamps used by the Moldavian post, in the period 1858–1865"*
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]], Planșa 18,
fig. 271–286, photo 139). Double-circle, roughly **21.5 × 12.2 mm**, with the
**post-office locality above and "MOLDOVA" below** in the exergue, the two words
separated by a short dash.

## History — Vienna, 1857

The dies were **ordered in the spring of 1857 at Vienna** (engraver **J.
Radnitzky**) by **Teodor Ghica, the diligence entrepreneur**, for the Moldavian
expedition bureaus under his management, and were invoiced in the inventory of **10
November 1857** deposited when the government bought back the "Expediția
Delijanselor" privilege and contract
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 139). They
were therefore in service **before** the *Cap de bour* stamps: Dragomir illustrates
a **pre-philatelic letter of 1857** bearing an early **`M1t`.JASSY/MOLDOVA 24/6**
(fig. 287, Dr. L. N. Cristea collection, photo 140–141). Once the stamps appeared,
these same devices became their principal obliterators — the M1 cancels that tie
the first issue to a town and date across the [[../postmarks/census|postmark
census]].

The fourteen bureaus of Planșa 18: **Bakeu, Berlad, Botuschani, Dorohoj, Fokschani,
Foltitscheni, Galatz, Husch, Jassy, Michaleni, Piatra, Roman, Tekutsch, Waslui**
(German-form town names) (photo 139).
