# 🐂 SM59 — Seal of the Iași Postal Bureau, United Principalities (1858/59)

The seal of the **Biroul Poștal Iași** issued at the **Union of the Principalities** —
and the moment the Moldavian *cap de bour* is **joined by the Wallachian eagle** in a
single device, under the legend **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE"** (the United Principalities).
It succeeds the [[SM58]] directorate seal
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 32, photo 21).

**Device (SM59, Fig. 32).** An **oval** seal, **33 × 30 mm**. In the field **two
accolated shields** — the **"cap de bour"** with, to its right, the **"vulturul"
(eagle)** — the shields **timbred with a closed crown** (five circles and a globe
with cross), the whole set on a **panoply of flags**; **below, the postal horn with
its bell to the left**. The legend in **Latin capitals**: **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE"**
(above) and **"DIRECȚIA BIROULUI POSTAL JASSY"** (1,5R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 32, photo 21).

**Variant SM59a (Fig. 33).** An **oval** seal, **33 × 30 mm**, struck **in ink**;
graphic composition identical to `SM59` (1,5R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 33, photo 21).

This double-arms design — bull's head **and** eagle — marks the constitutional shift
of 1859: the two principalities are now shown together, where [[SM58]] a year earlier
still bore Moldavia's arms alone. See [[../concepts/moldavia-heraldry]].
