# 🐂 SPM65 — Stamp of the Mustea postal enterprise (1865)

The stamp of the **Mustea postal enterprise** run by **Alexandru Mustea**, carrying
the **new united arms** in which the *cap de bour* and the eagle share a single
parted shield. Dragomir catalogues it as `SPM65` (illustrated on Plate 4 as
"Antrepriza Nustea")
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 47, photo 26).

**Device (Fig. 47).** A **round** stamp, **30 mm**. In the field the **new arms** —
a **parted shield (*scut despicat*)**: in the **first quarter the *cap de bour* with a
star between the horns**, in the **second quarter the eagle (*vulturul*)**. To the
right and left of the arms, the initials **"A.M." (Alexandru Mustea)**. The legend in
**Latin majuscules**: **"PRINCIPATELE UNITE"** (above) and, on two curved lines below,
**"Administrația Poștilor"** (small letters) / **"SECȚIUNILE IX, X, XII"**. Seen on
letters marked **"FRANCO"** or **"PORTO"** (15R)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 47, photo 26).
