# 🐂 SPM64 — Post-station stamp of Galbeni (1864)

The **1864 station stamp of Galbeni**, on the Roman–Adjud relay run. Unlike the
heraldic seals, it shows a **mounted courier** and the lessee's initials; Dragomir
records only a single surviving cover with it
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 46, photo 26).

**Device (Fig. 46).** A **square** stamp, **26 mm**, with **cut corners** (giving an
octagonal look). The field is divided in two: in the upper half, a **mounted
courier (*stafetar călare*)** with the **bag at his chest, the whip in the left hand
raised over the horse's tail in the wind and the reins in the right**. The legend in
**Latin majuscules**: **"POȘTA GALBINU"**, below it an **oval cartouche with two
neo-Gothic letters "G.R."**. To the left of the shield (as viewed), the year split in
two: **18 / 64**. Struck in **green and greenish-brown ink**, over **1864–1868**
(15R) ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] Fig. 46,
photo 26).

**History.** Dragomir notes **"G.R." = Gheorghe Răzmeriță**, who on **25 February
1865** became the lessee of the **Galbeni – Capu Dracului – Berești** posts, linking
**Roman and Adjud**. A **single "porto" cover** addressed to Iași is known, the mark
in green ink (collection **Fr. Heimbüchler**)
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photo 26).
