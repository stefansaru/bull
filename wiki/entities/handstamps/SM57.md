# 🐂 SM57 — Directorate seal of the Moldavian posts (1857)

A **directorate-level Moldavian postal seal of 1857**. It is recorded in Dragomir's
concordance grid **TABEL I (Ștampilele sigilare ale poștei din Moldova, 1822–1858)**,
where the **`SM57`** column carries a mark **only in the *Direcția* (directorate)
row** — i.e. it was a seal of the central directorate at Iași, not of the district
bureaus ([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] TABEL I,
photo 13).

**Device.** ⚠ (OCR — verify) Dragomir's running catalogue (§1.1.2) gives no separate
descriptive paragraph or figure for `SM57`; it appears **only as a column in TABEL
I**, sequenced between [[SM56]] (Administrația Poștelor, 1856) and [[SM58]] (Direcția
Poștelor și a Delijanselor, 1858). Its exact form, size and legend are therefore not
established here and should be confirmed against a legible impression or a fuller
reading of the catalogue text
([[../../sources/dragomir-stampilografie-1990/text|Ștampilografie]] photos 13, 19–20).
