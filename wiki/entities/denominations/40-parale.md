# 🐃 40 parale — Second issue (1858)

The **ordinary single-letter** value of the **second *Cap de bour* issue**
([[../issues/issue-bull-2]]). Catalogued **L.P. 6** (= Yvert 6 = Michel 6x = Scott 6)
([[../references/compilations/bull-head-catalogue-valuations|valuations]]).

## Design & paper
**Blue / greenish-blue** on **azuré (bluish) / white / yellowish paper**; the bull's
head with a post-horn ([[../census/bull-head-evidence-census|census]]). A **Type I
1858 original die** is distinguished from later states
([[../references/catalogues/kiriac-1974|Kiriac 1974]] pp. 33, 47, via
[[../census/bull-head-evidence-census]]). Multiples are recorded — strips of four and
a **tête-bêche** vertical strip (Mihăileni) ([[../census/bull-head-evidence-census]]).

## Printing & survival
Total print run **106,032** — by far the largest of any Bull's Head value, which is
why the 40 parale is the **commonest** of the whole series
([[../references/compilations/bull-head-catalogue-valuations]]).

## Valuation (unused / used)
- Spineanu 1984: **2 500 / 1 800** (white/grey); **3 000 / 2 500** (blue paper) Lei
- Cârjan-Manole 2006: **450 / 350 EUR** (white base)
- Chiricheș-Pascariu: **464 / 464** (white); **833 / 531** (blue) (unit ⚠)
