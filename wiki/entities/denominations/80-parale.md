# 🐃 80 parale — Second issue (1858) — registered rate

The top value of the **second *Cap de bour* issue** ([[../issues/issue-bull-2]]),
covering the **registered-letter fee**. Catalogued **L.P. 7** (= Yvert 7 = Michel
7x = Scott 7) ([[../references/compilations/bull-head-catalogue-valuations|valuations]]).

## Design & paper
The bull's head with a post-horn, **white / brick-red (*cărămiziu*)** on white paper
(1858/59), and — the key variety — a printing on **azure paper (1861)**
([[../census/bull-head-evidence-census|census]]). The 80-parale fee is documented
paid *by other values too* — e.g. a pair of 40 parale covering the 80-para
registered fee with a RECEPISSE mark ([[../handstamps/C1]];
[[../census/bull-head-evidence-census]]).

## Printing & survival
Total print run **60,072** ([[../references/compilations/bull-head-catalogue-valuations]]). The
**azure-paper (1861)** printing is the standout rarity of the value.

## Valuation (unused / used)
- Spineanu 1984: **5 500 / 3 000** (white); **250 000 / 7 500** (azure) Lei
- Cârjan-Manole 2006: **850 / 700** (white); **10 500–11 500 / 4 000–5 000** (azure) EUR
- Chiricheș-Pascariu: **1 498 / 475** (white); **21 301 / 5 759** (azure) (unit ⚠)

The white/brick-red base is inexpensive; the **azure 1861** paper is among the great
rarities of the second issue.
