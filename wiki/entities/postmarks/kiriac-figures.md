# Kiriac figure catalogue — classic postmarks (1858)

> **Concept stage** ([[../../meta/decisions/DR-011-raw-source-ingestion-stage|DR-011]]): structured from the raw [[../../sources/kiriac-1974/chapter-1-postmarks-1858|Kiriac 1974 Chapter I]] text — each Kiriac figure → marking type, towns, dimensions, ink, period. Romanian diacritics restored from context; every row cites its Kiriac pdf page ([[../references/catalogues/kiriac-1974]], DR-007). `status: draft` until verified against the source images.

This is the authoritative spine for the [[census|auction postmark census]] and for the [[../handstamps/PM52|PM52]]/[[../handstamps/M1|M1]]/[[../handstamps/M2|M2]]/[[../handstamps/M3|M3]]/[[../handstamps/C1|C1]] → Kiriac concordance.

> ⚠ **Two Kiriac numbering systems — do not conflate.** The figure numbers here are from the **1974 catalogue, Chapter I** (Fig. 37–119). The `Kiriac fig. 271–313` numbers cited in the auction [[census]] are from Dragomir's later ***Ștampilografie poștală* (1990)** — a *different* numbering of the **same devices**. Map the two by **device type + town** (e.g. the B.1 round *MOLDOVA* Iași datestamp here = the auction "JASSY / MOLDOVA" = *Ștampilografie* fig. 279), not by figure number. Confirm against the 1990 work when available.

**14 marking groups** derived from Kiriac pdf pages 28–42. **5** are directly relevant to cancelling the 1858 Bull's Head stamps.

## Notes

- **Fig. 37** (clișee-peceți rotunde ale mărcilor / round printing dies (clichés) of the stamps): NOT a postmark — the four round steel dies used to print the 1858 Cap de Bour emission I at Atelia Timbrului, Iași. Each has two notches to fix it in the press. Confecționarea cliseelor a costat 389 lei. 24.000 mărci imprimate.
- **Fig. 39** (clișeu PORTO GAZETEI (neutilizat) / PORTO GAZETEI die (unused)): NOT a postmark — a round unused stamp die discovered 1944 at Fabrica de Timbre, București. Legend PORTO GAZETEI in Latin letters, bour with 5-point star, value '10 P' (parale) in the horn's loop. Proposed 12 Sept 1858 for 10-parale newspaper stamps; never produced (newspaper tax reduced to 5 parale).
- **Fig. 40-54** (ștampilă rotundă cu cerc dublu și dată (MOLDOVA) / round double-circle datestamp (MOLDOVA)): The 15 Moldovan datestamps, made in Vienna with German-orthography town names; outer circle name of office, MOLDOVA below in Egyptian majuscules, date centre as fraction (day/month) in Arabic numerals. JASSY has 2 types (2nd with MOLDOVA in antiqua/sans-serif, not seen on 1858 stamps). Some found on archive documents/unfranked covers dated well before 15 July 1858, hence also pre-philatelic (early dates: JASSY 24/6 and WASLUI 4/11, both 1857). Offices ADJUD, TG. NEAMȚ, TG. OCNA, TG. FRUMOS handled stamps but had no 1858 handstamp (cancelled at district).
- **Fig. 55** (ștampilă baston RECEPISSE (fără chenar) / straight-line RECEPISSE handstamp (registered mail)): Frameless straight-line 'RECEPISSE' in oblique Latin majuscules, applied to covers together with the round MOLDOVA datestamp to denote registered (recipisate/strahovoi) letters. Grouped by letter height: ~5 mm (Bacău, Botoșani, Dorohoi, Fălticeni, Huși, Iași, Mihăileni, Piatra, Roman, Vaslui); ~4 mm very inclined (Bârlad, Iași, Tg. Neamț, Tg. Ocna); ~3 mm (Galați). Continued in use in the 2nd emission alongside B.3/FRANCO.
- **Fig. 57-74** (ștampilă FRANCO cu chenar / framed FRANCO handstamp): Word 'FRANCO' in a frame whose shape differs per office 'spre a se stârpi abuzurile cu refolosirea mărcilor' (M.F. nr. 361, 30 Nov 1859). Corrects the erroneous 'PORTO' printed on the stamps. Applied on the stamp itself, paired with the round MOLDOVA datestamp (B.1/B.2) on the cover. IAȘI and GALAȚI each used 2 types. Rare: JASSY (tip 2) and MIHĂILENI. Continued in use into the Principatele Unite period.
- **Fig. 75-80** (ștampilă rotundă mare MOLDOVA cu dată / large round double-circle MOLDOVA datestamp): Ten additional larger round double-circle datestamps (outer 25 mm, inner 13 mm) of the same MOLDOVA type but with taller/fuller 4 mm letters; town above, two demarcation flowers, MOLDOVA below, date centre as ordinary fraction. JASSY tip 2 (acanthus leaf) for simple/registered on arrival; JASSY tip 1 (narrow flower) for registered-value letters.
- **Fig. 81-82** (ștampilă FOKSCHANI fără MOLDOVA / FOKSCHANI datestamp without the word MOLDOVA): The small B.1/FOKSCHANI datestamp (from 3 Jan 1862) and the large B.2/FOKSCHANI datestamp (from 30 Dec 1861) appeared with the word 'MOLDOVA' removed, becoming B.4 (Fig. 81, ex-B.1) and B.5 (Fig. 82, ex-B.2), used until June 1865. Late date: B.5 on 20 PARALE 'Cuza' 23/2.
- **Fig. 85-89** (ștampilă cu agrafă / clasp (agrafă) datestamp): Double circle 'cu agrafă' (simple clasp) datestamps, office name in Egyptian capitals, date centre as fraction, clasp below. Appear only in Muntenia. București had 2 types: Tip I round clasp knot, smaller wider letters; Tip II oval clasp knot, large 3.5 mm letters. Used to cancel Principatele Unite (1862) stamps, not the Bull's Head.
- **Fig. 90-98, 92a-92f** (ștampilă cu stea / star datestamp): The ordinary 'cu stea' (star) double-circle datestamp of Muntenia; office name in Egyptian capitals, date as fraction, star below. STIRBEIU is the postmark name of the town Călărași. Cancel Principatele Unite stamps.
- **Fig. 99-100** (ștampilă stea cu punct / star-with-dot datestamp): Same as B.9 but the star is a dot with six linear rays. Muntenia only; cancels Principatele Unite stamps.
- **Fig. 101-102** (ștampilă cu draperie JASSY / JASSY drapery cancel): Original 'cu draperie' cancels, rectangle with cut (chamfered) corners; 'JASSY' in Roman Didot majuscules on a tasselled drapery banderole; date on three lines in Arabic numerals with the year shown by its last two digits — the first Romanian postmark to indicate the year. Two forms: B.6 (larger) and B.7 (smaller). Also found on 20 PARALE/1865 'Cuza'.
- **Fig. 103** (ștampilă RECOM (fără chenar) / frameless RECOM(andat) registered handstamp): Frameless straight-line 'RECOM(andat)' abbreviation in five Egyptian capital letters 5 mm high, introduced in Muntenia in 1862 to certify registered mail (successor role to C.3/RECEPISSE).
- **Fig. ** (ștampilă P.P. (porto plătit) / P.P. (porto-paid) handstamp): Majuscule 'P.P.' in a 15×9 mm rectangle. Common in Muntenia during 1 May–26 June 1862 when no postage stamps circulated. Later, up to 1864, P.P. stamps used also in Moldova, alone or combined with round or FRANCO handstamps. No accompanying figure.
- **Fig. ** (ștampilă RETUR / RETUR handstamp): Frameless 29×14 mm handstamp with 'P.P. Retur' in Gothic characters, used at the București post office. No accompanying figure number (shown as a 'PP' image).
