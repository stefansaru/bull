# Glossary — Symbols & Abbreviations

> For specialist **terms** (datestamp, laid paper, tête-bêche, franco, cover…) in EN/RO,
> see the companion [[terminology]] glossary. This page is the book's own **symbols &
> abbreviations** legend.
>
> Verbatim from the book's own legend (Table 1: Symbols, Table 2: Abbreviations).
> This is the author's controlled vocabulary. The emoji act as **semantic markers**
> used throughout the narrative headings (e.g. `📅` date, `💰` currency, `🧭` map/geopolitics,
> `📯` postal, `🐃` aurochs, `🌍` place, `📖` catalogue, `⨀` cancellation/handstamp).
> Do not invent new markers; extend this table instead. See [[ontology]].
