## Context
The user added ~14 reference works (RO/FR/EN) to build the **context layer** and asked
to "paint a picture of why the Bull's Heads are meaningful." Four directions were
confirmed.

## Decision
1. **Thesis — heritage-led, all woven.** The emotional spine is **national identity /
   memory**: an ancient emblem (the aurochs) becomes a nation's first postal voice on the
   eve of Union. The **philatelic romance** (a humble object made priceless) and the
   **detective story** (forgeries, plating, aurochs-vs-wisent) are woven in as supporting
   threads, not the lead.
2. **Enrichment shape — curated & deep.** A bounded first pass of **~15–25 well-developed
   thematic concept pages** on the named themes, richly cited — not exhaustive mining of
   all ~3,500 source pages. Breadth can grow later.
3. **Stories — legends labeled.** Tell the great stories (Dragoș & the aurochs, etc.) but
   **explicitly distinguish legend/tradition from documented fact** (convention C-17), each
   attributed. Color *and* credibility.
4. **Packaging — wiki-first.** Develop the context deeply in the wiki against the existing
   chapter skeleton; decide book packaging (one richer book / context+catalogue split /
   series) **later**, once the volume is visible. No premature packaging.
5. **Present-day — web-supplemented.** Facts the pre-1975 sources can't cover (current
   state of buildings, modern record prices, living collectors, present scarcity) are
   filled by cited web research alongside the documents.

## Consequences
- Concept pages carry the heritage framing and cross-link the three threads.
- New convention **C-17 (legend vs. fact labeling)**; audit checks it.
- A `strategy/enrichment-plan.md` lists the target theme pages and their sources.
- Editorial latitude here is **sourced synthesis** (not verbatim) — distinct from the
  book-narrative verbatim baseline (DR-004); these are new concept pages, always cited.
