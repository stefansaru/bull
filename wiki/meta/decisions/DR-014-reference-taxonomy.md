# DR-014 — References are one type with kinds; images point to their source

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Status | accepted |
| Date | 2026-10-25 |
| Mode | CONCEPT / MAINTENANCE |
| Actor(s) | user ("organize different types of artefacts: auctions, catalogues, images, books, references, links … perhaps all subcategories of references") + agent (four choices clarified) |
| Related | DR-002 (one entity, one file), DR-007 (references for claims), DR-011 (raw sources), DR-012 (source translations) |

## Context

The artefacts the wiki relies on had drifted into an unstructured mix:
- `entities/references/` held 16 auction catalogues, 4 stamp catalogues, 2 monographs and 2 compiled pages side by side, told apart only by file names.
- `entities/catalogues/`, planned for the numbering systems (Michel, Scott, LP…), was empty.
- 21 raw works sat under `sources/` and 14 of them had no reference page at all.
- The 1,049 book images and the figure crops under `assets/sources/` recorded no link to the work they came from.
- About 300 URLs in the prose pointed at sites the wiki said nothing about.

Two things now depend on this being orderly. DR-007 needs every claim to land on an identifiable reference. The app edition needs image rights decided before it can be sold, and the realistic unit of a rights decision is the *source* (a book, a sale, a museum), not the single image.

## Decision

1. **One entity type, `reference`, with a kind.** Each kind has its own folder under `entities/references/`, and each page carries `ref_type:`.

   | Folder | `ref_type` values | Holds |
   |--------|-------------------|-------|
   | `books/` | `book`, `bibliography` | monographs, histories, documentary collections |
   | `catalogues/` | `catalogue-series`, `catalogue-edition` | numbering systems (Michel, Scott, Yvert, SG, LP) and the editions cited; an edition links its series via `series:` |
   | `auctions/` | `auction` | one page per sale |
   | `articles/` | `article`, `blog-post`, `web-article`, `notes` | articles; `in:` links the journal, book or site |
   | `web/` | `website`, `database`, `digital-library`, `auction-house`, `marketplace`, `blog`, `web-archive` | one page per site, with `domains:` and `reliability:` |
   | `archives/` | `heritage-register`, `museum`, … | archival fonds, museums, registers |
   | `compilations/` | `compilation` | pages this wiki compiles from several references |

   `entities/references/README.md` is the index. It is generated by `tools/gen_references_index.py`.

2. **Images are not references; each one points to its source.**
   - In `assets/rights.yml` an image may carry `source: references/<kind>/<slug>` and a `locator:` (page, figure or lot).
   - A reference page may declare `assets:` (the path prefixes of images taken from it) and `rights_default:` (`status`, `licence`, `note`).
   - An image whose own status is `pending` inherits its source's default. Clearing or omitting a whole source is therefore one edit.
   - An image's own status always wins over the default.
   - `--rights-init` fills an empty `source` from the `assets:` prefixes and never changes a decision.

3. **Lots live inside their auction page.** Each `auctions/` page has a *Bull's Head lots* table (page, issue, denomination, on cover, certificates, description), generated by `tools/gen_census_wiki.py`. `compilations/bulls-head-lots` remains the curated cross-sale selection.

4. **URLs stay inline; each site gets one page.**
   - The prose keeps its links.
   - Every site that is cited repeatedly gets a `web/` or `archives/` page saying what it is and how far to trust it.
   - `tools/link_report.py` counts URLs per domain, separates raw-OCR footers in `sources/` from real citations, and lists the domains without a page.

## Alternatives considered

- **A flat folder with `ref_type` only**: rejected. More than 60 files in one folder hide the kinds the user asked to see.
- **Separate top-level types** (auction, catalogue, book, website): rejected. The user framed them as subcategories of references, and DR-007's "cite a reference" stays one rule.
- **One page per image**: rejected. That is more than 1,000 pages, each carrying no information beyond its source, and rights would still be decided per source in practice.
- **One page per lot**: deferred. It would help to follow one item across sales (provenance chains); revisit when the census needs it.
- **Converting every URL to a wikilink**: rejected for now. It would touch hundreds of `verbatim` pages (DR-004).

## Consequences

- 25 pages moved and 101 files had their links rewritten. The broken-link count is unchanged: 37 before, 37 after, all pre-existing.
- 38 new reference pages, 7 of them marked `needs-identification` (author, date or origin still to be recorded). A page with that status cannot back a claim (DR-007).
- `entities/catalogues/` is removed. Catalogue series are `references/catalogues/` pages.
- The rights work becomes a per-source checklist; the wizard report counts images by source.
- 69% of prose URLs now land on a site page. Run `tools/link_report.py wiki` to see the rest.
- Follow-up: record `source` for the 1,049 book images (`assets/media/`). Most are crops from the references above, but nothing in the files says which.
