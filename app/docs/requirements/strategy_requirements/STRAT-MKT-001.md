# STRAT-MKT-001 — Paid upfront

## Intent
New apps in the catalogue default to paid; a book suits a one-time price.

## Requirement
The app is sold at a one-time price on Google Play; the price is set by the owner before the first release.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Price decided and recorded
- [ ] Paid app configured in Play Console

## Verification
Owner checklist.
