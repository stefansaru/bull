# STRAT-VAL-002 — Clear and intuitive

## Intent
Readers must never wonder where they are or how to get back.

## Requirement
Every screen follows the platform navigation model; reading is at most two interactions from launch.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Continue reading on the contents screen
- [ ] Hallway test with a first-time reader

## Verification
APP-UX-001.
