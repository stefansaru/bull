# STRAT-VAL-003 — Respect the reader: no data, no ads

## Intent
A paid book owes its reader silence: no tracking, no ads, no upsell.

## Requirement
The app shall contain no advertising, no in-app purchases and no data collection.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] No ads, IAP or analytics dependencies

## Verification
pubspec review.
