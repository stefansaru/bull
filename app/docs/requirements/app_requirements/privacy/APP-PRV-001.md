# APP-PRV-001 — Privacy notice in the app

## Intent
Even an app that collects nothing must say so.

## Requirement
Settings shows the privacy summary and the policy URL; the policy page is published at that URL.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Summary and URL in Settings
- [ ] Policy page published

## Verification
Manual.
