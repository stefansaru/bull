# APP-UX-002 — Paper-and-ink design

## Intent
The look should recall the 1858 stamps: cream laid paper, black and blue handstamps.

## Requirement
Light theme on cream paper, dark theme, seed colour from the stamps; a serif reading face is a v1 candidate.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Light and dark themes
- [ ] Designed icon and TV banner
- [ ] Reading typeface decided

Design review.
