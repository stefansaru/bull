# NORM-GPDP-01 — Google Play paid-app obligations

## Intent
A paid app needs a payments profile, an accurate listing and a working privacy policy; misleading metadata leads to rejection.

## Requirement
The store listing shall describe the content accurately (including that some images may be omitted), declare no data collection truthfully, link the privacy policy, and the developer account shall have a payments profile for paid apps.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Payments profile set up on the personal Play Console
- [ ] Listing text, screenshots per form factor, content rating questionnaire
- [ ] Data safety form: no data collected
- [ ] Privacy policy URL live
